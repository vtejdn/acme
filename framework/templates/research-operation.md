# Research Operation

Before creating or retaining content with this template, apply the [common content decision](../rules/records.md#substantive-material-retention), reuse an adequate responsible home, and then follow this kind's actual registration, reproduction, source or review obligations.
A template does not justify a process-only record.

### Compact Research-Operation Template

Use this for a [substantive operation or useful attempt](../rules/ledger.md#substantive-mathematical-operations), not every scratch calculation.
An exploratory entry may remain in its existing responsible section.
Fill version transitions, consumers, reviews and authorization only when they apply; a missing Claim or completed review does not prevent recording a useful attempt.
Internal extraction needs no separate approval; for main-theorem designation or objective changes, apply the [result-organization policy](../rules/claims.md#lemma-extraction-and-approval); this field does not create an approval requirement for every operation.

```markdown
# <Mathematical operation or coherent batch>
Record kind: research operation
Date and owner: <actual date and responsible human; actual agent contribution when relevant>
Context: <authorized question, Task or route; existing affected Claims/proof versions>
Reason: <mathematical purpose>
Authorization, when required: <applicable existing instruction or approval>

## Mathematical Change or Attempt
<Before/after statements or proof structure where applicable;
for exploration, state the attempted question, provisional assumptions and discriminating step.>

## Evidence and Outcome
<Actual calculations, arguments or checks and their limitations; link artifacts and reviews when present.
Retain attached programs and data themselves, embedded or linked from retained files, for successful and failed substantive explorations.
Include command/environment, parameters, outcome/failure, limits and next action; a narrative alone is insufficient.
No separate commit, locator or hash is required.
Identify observations, conditional deductions and gaps.>

## Versions, Consumers and Dated History, When Applicable
<Existing version transitions, preserved originals, current-map owner, affected consumers and dated decisions.
An attempt without such transitions records only its relevant outcome and next action.
Keep provenance separate from logical inputs.>
```

Use this template only for substantive mathematical operations or findings.
Routine naming, path, link and layout maintenance needs no operation entry or separate correspondence report.

Keep continuing unbound arguments in rolling `mathematics/drafts/Author_D_F0001.md` material.
Preserve a useful failed finding at its fixed mathematical home when it informs continuation, with exact related route/Claim/method scope.
Programs and native data stay with their J group.
Omit irrelevant relations.
After promotion and consumer repair, remove only the corresponding completed draft, retaining genuinely continuing work and meaningful failed mathematics.

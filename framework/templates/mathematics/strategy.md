# Strategy

## Route Record Template

Apply the [work-obligation rules](../../rules/workflow.md#work-obligations) and [agent/tool responsibility](../../rules/workflow.md#agent-and-tool-responsibilities).
The optional `material.py create --kind S --line F --file route.md` writer, or an actual `--task PATH`, allocates the supplied route.
Associate S with the actual ordinary individual T; a joint coordination T has no direct S or E.
Replace illustrative paths with actual material locators; omit absent related claims and inapplicable fields.
An exploratory route can begin with a question and explicitly provisional scope.

```markdown
---
author: Author
---
# Short route title

## Task associations
| Task | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| [Actual Task contract](../tasks/Author_T_F0001_v1.md#contract) | Exact part of the authorized question pursued here. |

## Route
| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| disposition | proposed |
| priority | Actual relative priority and reason. |
| parent | [Actual coordinating S](ACTUAL_PARENT_PATH#route) |

Related claims, when present: links to actual Claim material
Use an actual UTC-minute date only for a substantive decision or dated fact requiring retention; preserve genuinely known historical calendar precision.
Ordinary allocation, pause/restart and parent maintenance update current arrangements and necessary reasons without a dated change entry.
Omit the parent row for a root; replace the illustrative locator when used.
Historical lineage, when applicable: predecessor / successors and the dated formulation and obligations actually inherited.
A current parent is not lineage.
For a received predecessor: precise source formulation and obligations actually carried here;
preserve the source disposition, necessary formulation and carried obligations without changing the source.

## Current Question or Precise Target
Identify the exploratory question, mechanism or range to test and mark provisional assumptions.
For a particular proof target, state its exact hypotheses, quantifiers and conclusion.
When claiming a necessary, sufficient or equivalent relation to the goal, give its justification.
Mark provisional implications and partial targets explicitly.
For example, estimate A would settle the bounded-parameter case, while the full target also needs an unproved uniform estimate B.

## Inputs and Obstruction
Identify verified inputs, with links where available, and state which premises or applicability checks remain open.
Explain the current obstruction and the conditions carried into any exploratory conclusion.
For example, a family may rule out the uniformity needed by a normalization while leaving the original proposition open.

## Next Step and Decision Conditions
State the next step and how its result could change the decision to continue or redirect.
In the example with A and B, test the feasibility of B before fully developing A and retain any established bounded-parameter result for its local use.
After the normalization failure, look for a mechanism that addresses the failed uniformity.
Renaming the variables leaves the same obstruction.
For paused or blocked work, give the reason, outstanding obligation and restart condition.

## Reuse and Branch Roles (when consequential)
Explain reused constructions through local objects, satisfied/missing hypotheses, actual output and remaining gap, with exact evidence.
Retain consequential losses and order of choices.
One sentence suffices for a simple application.
A coordinating route explains how its independently managed branches contribute:
alternatives, joint components or a mixture.
The child owns its current parent, local next step and disposition; do not copy child histories into a second table.

## Evidence and Changes
Links to substantive ledger attempts, computations, and audits.
Retain necessary mathematical reasons, useful failure scope, consequential decisions and carried obligations, linking adequate existing evidence.
Current disposition, priority, parent, unfinished work and next action belong in their current sections, without a per-operation agent, date or before/after stream.
For a major redirection or stage closeout: link the original dated expectation,
actual result, explanation of the difference and resulting decision.
Keep concise reusable lessons here and complete cases in their responsible mathematical evidence.
Keep the same rolling S through substantial progress or local repairs when its mechanism and intended contribution survive.
Allocate a new S only for a distinct mechanism or independently managed branch;
preserve its actual dated origin, without inventing predecessor history for a new child.
For a received original, record consequential feedback or derivation here with the precise source formulation, applicability judgment and necessary fixed evidence.
This is complete without editing the source or waiting for its author's response;
matching authorship alone does not make a received original writable.
```

Use the [defined dispositions](../../rules/strategies.md#route-dispositions), including `paused`, in the route itself; the index derives those fields.
Keep evidence, reasons and lineage in the affected route records;
the stable mathematics entrance and other navigation must not maintain competing priorities or dispositions.

Apply the [route ownership boundary](../../rules/strategies.md#writable-route-boundary) and [hierarchy contract](../../rules/strategies.md#strategy-hierarchy).
Explicit source references support reverse discovery without implying source adoption or supersession.
Omit inapplicable sections; no replacement knowledge registry, version chain or retrospective is required for every ordinary action.

Responsibility for the strategy body and layout belongs to the established author.
Under the [locator-only maintenance rule](../../rules/identity.md#collaboration-maintenance-and-conflicts), an agent with maintenance authority may repair a path or anchor in another author's local writable S after confirming that the repair preserves the actual target, mathematical meaning and scope of adoption and review.
Keep the existing S, its author, provenance, evidence, route decisions, Task associations and actual reviews, and repair affected downstream references under that rule.
The responsible author handles substantive revisions and adoption under the applicable rules.
Other authors put substantive corrections and contributions in their own suitable materials with exact references.
Coordinate concurrent writes and maintain shared registration/navigation at their actual scope.
Preserve acquired-source provenance and historical mathematics.

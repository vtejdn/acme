# Source Unit

Before creating or retaining content, apply the [common content decision](../../rules/records.md#substantive-material-retention), reuse an adequate responsible home, and follow the [source-audit rules](../../rules/literature-audits.md).
This template supplies no authorization, completed inspection or mandatory field checklist.

Use mathematics/audits/literature/SourceKey/Author_U001.md for an author-owned rolling unit, without _vN.
Numbers are independent for each source key and author, with actual external origins distinguished; Yin_U001.md and Ma_U001.md may coexist.
Maintain the author-specific [allocation state](../../material-format.md#material-family-allocation-state) and keep file-local anchors stable.
The short author/provenance header follows the [material format](../../material-format.md#fixed-source-unit-inputs).

Link the actual inspected edition explanation in the source README and give precise passages, hypotheses, conventions, mathematical content and actual inspection limits in ordinary prose.
Source notes may have useful author sections but no required per-author fields, holding ledger or acquisition account.
Changing the current original link does not change an earlier inspection's edition.
No Source and inspection table, coverage-state enumeration, fingerprint or per-edit log is required.

Reuse complete definitions at adequate U/C/L homes and register precise stable entries through the [definition index rules](../../rules/definitions.md).
Substantive project deductions retain their responsible fixed material.
Maintain actual downstream references when changing the unit, preserving the mathematical object, edition and adoption/review scope; later checks do not become old R evidence.
Associated Lean code is outside the [default scope](../../rules/literature-audits.md#default-audit-scope) unless explicitly requested.

The following is an adaptable example, not an allocated unit or completed check:

```markdown
---
author: <actual registered human>
---
# <Author>: <source finding>

## <unit title>

Use the [actual preprint edition](readme.md#actual-edition), Section <locator>.
State the exact theorem/definition, quantifiers, domains and hypotheses here.

Explain source notation, normalization and local conversions with their justification.
Reuse complete definitions and distinguish theorem assertions from definitions.

Describe the proof mechanism and actual reading/checking depth.
Identify dependencies, uninspected steps, genuine reviewer relationship and useful failures or limits.
Keep known inspection dates at their actual precision without inventing unknown history.

Explain the intended application and its exact adopted range.
Link necessary project arguments at their responsible homes.
```

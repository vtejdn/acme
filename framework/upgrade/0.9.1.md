## What changed

- **Origins and scopes.** The intake/action rules, material format, `scripts/acme/intake/acquire.py` and `addresses.py` require explicit `origin: current` for selected current-repository C/R/L/J repairs or U contributions.
  Current J group and selected native-file provenance use the same actual fixed snapshot; unchanged inherited scope retains its original acquisition facts.
  Distinct scopes of one U anchor may supply distinct receiving units without invented source anchors.
- **Historical L and scoped views.** The Git-view and affected document/navigation/preparation/render/runtime/Task readers separate historical recorded L locators from actually selected current anchors, and qualify only necessary WORKTREE candidates or unambiguous INDEX stage-0 inputs.
  The selected-U check stays within its actual namespace; unrelated deleted or unmerged files do not become a whole-tree gate.
- **Responsibilities.** Record, Task, workflow and action guidance unify research repair, explicit maintenance stages, independent acceptance and affected-author cleanup without retrospective items for completed incidental repairs.
  Personal transfer still needs its actual human authorization, and mathematical Task completion remains separate from manuscript delivery.
- **Upgrades and local pages.** Versioning, organization and action rules protect a locally authored publication page even at `user-manual/publication-example.md`, and retain explicit human limits on scoped shared-repository upgrade delivery.
  An authorized upgrade in an actual multi-author shared Git repository includes scoped commit, push and remote-result confirmation under the existing delivery rules, subject to explicit local-only, offline or no-push limits.
  Enabled non-offline official-query permission failures use host permission handling and one permitted recheck, with existing `--force` when today's unavailable cache would suppress it.
- **Human documents.** The six English, Chinese, French, German, Japanese and Spanish manuals under `user-manual/` and six `user-manual/user-instructions/*.md` guides carry supplied changes.
  This release's ordinary-upgrade guidance limits human-document maintenance to necessary authorized version/link and comparable mechanical edits, preserving destination-authored bodies.
  `scripts/readme.md` describes the corrected interfaces.

Only this upgrade note is added; no earlier shipped path is removed or moved.

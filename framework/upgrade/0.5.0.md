- **Computation reception.** J `output_attachments` and its source-output reads, transfer, mapping, outcome and onward mechanisms are removed.

- **Local execution.** `scripts/acme/execution/records.py`, Task checks, `.gitignore`, execution/Task rules and their templates make `execution/` persistent local state excluded from Git synchronization.
  Task creation/import does not create E, Task show no longer embeds it, and the explicit E checker has no Git `--view` or historical fallback.
- **Sources, collaboration and setup.** Literature helpers and literature/reuse/search guidance keep unregistered local PDFs silent, prepare only actual source needs and do not invent acquisition dates.

  The six manuals under `user-manual/` in English, Chinese, French, German, Japanese and Spanish update only their version sentence to `0.5.0` in this release.

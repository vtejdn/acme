## Record concrete literature follow-up

When authorized work establishes a concrete unresolved literature acquisition, missing passage, locator, attribution, edition or necessary source-verification need that requires later handling, record it in `mathematics/maintenance.md`.
This applies during research, writing, source preparation, receipt and assigned material checks, including working Drafts and source checks for known results with sufficient local proofs.
The issue need not already violate a conformity rule or block the current proof.
Do not leave its only follow-up entrance in a Draft, Preparation, source note, handoff or ordinary reply.
An agent with restricted write scope supplies the concrete issue to the responsible agent rather than exceeding that scope or claiming registration already happened.

Reuse an existing item for the same actual need, keeping affected uses and unresolved author portions clear.
Use concise ordinary prose with the known work or source lead, affected material and purpose, unresolved need, represented human, affected local author and next action or actual limitation.
An unresolved identification need not have a complete bibliography entry, and ordinary preparation needs no invented rule violation.
Shared entries retain repository-relative locators and shareable facts without credentials, host paths, private-account links or local execution contents.

Keep bibliography at its canonical home, edition/source explanations in adequate source notes, inspected mathematics and limits in U or adequate materials, and adoption or mathematical obligations at their consumers.
Maintenance coordinates remaining action; it does not own evidence or replace necessary mathematical qualifications.
Do not create U, L or R merely to record a follow-up item.

Complete straightforward preparation directly when possible; no retrospective item is needed for a resolved intermediate step.
A possible future reading interest or absent local PDF without an actual further preparation need creates no issue by itself.
Routine startup, navigation, Git delivery and upgrades do not inventory all local originals or initiate downloads.

Continue directly reporting actual acquisition failure and preserving useful source limitations at their responsible homes.

## Handle and clear by actual work

Ordinary literature lookup, acquisition, location and verification follow the literature rules and actual authority.
An entry alone requires no prior conformity inspector, isolated repair agent, independent conformity acceptance or new formal review.
After completing and checking the actual need and maintaining necessary source facts and consumers, the responsible local author's agent may confirm and clear that author's resolved portion within its authority.
Adequate verified alternatives or an authorized actual change of use may resolve a need only with the necessary consumer handling and preservation of still-needed evidence.
Failure, silence, temporary non-use or reporting alone does not resolve an outstanding need.

Preserve other-author, unknown-author, unresolved, out-of-scope and concurrent portions and necessary common context.
Use the existing receiving-side disposition boundary where applicable; a cited paper's external author is not required to register or approve local literature preparation.

Actual conformity repairs retain their existing independent acceptance and applicable stage isolation.

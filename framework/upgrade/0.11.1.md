# Upgrade from 0.11.0 to 0.11.1

The preceding official tag `0.11.0` identifies [commit 333cf2eac79b94553c04379b1e0a9cbb92f70ef8](https://github.com/vtejdn/acme/commit/333cf2eac79b94553c04379b1e0a9cbb92f70ef8).
The target is `0.11.1`, a compatible development upgrade: ordinary synchronization and bounded merges of local edits suffice without substantive maintenance of existing materials, project configuration or integrations.

## Changes

- **Registration before the startup question.** `framework/rules/workflow.md`, `framework/rules/tasks.md` and `framework/agent-actions.md` require an unanswered mathematical question that needs sustained research to be registered or matched to a proposed Task before asking whether to begin.
  Existing authorization for the same objective and scope goes directly to the applicable setup or continuation path.
  Registration preserves the boundary between recording a question and authorizing research.
- **Approved research startup.** The research entrance explicitly leads the responsible agent to the existing main-agent, Task/E, host-continuity and coordination duties.
  The main agent enables or reuses a supported and permitted host goal, loop or equivalent facility and arranges ordinary research agents or agent-S when their work would help the actual objective.
  Effective stops, resource limits, host requirements and existing execution ownership continue to govern that work.
- **Established terminology and precise descriptions.** The general writing rules in `framework/rules/writing.md` prohibit fabricated technical terminology and improvised combinations that merely look like terms.
  When no established name is known, describe the object precisely using established mathematical concepts, its defining properties and relations, and necessary notation.
  Definition naming in `framework/rules/definitions.md` and the existing writing self-check and review standard apply the same requirement, including inherited and provisional expressions and their subsequent uses.
- **Essential abstracts and readable proofs.** The common mathematical writing rules in `framework/rules/writing.md` require abstracts to emphasize the core problem, principal conclusions and substantive contribution, with essential conditions and the key method or mechanism where useful.
  Proof exposition must combine mathematical correctness, precision and readability, retaining symbols, formulas and intermediate calculations that help the intended reader follow the argument.
  The notation and formula/prose guidance and existing writing self-checks and assigned writing reviews apply the same requirements at their actual scope and review kind.
- **Computation outputs and necessary fixed inputs.** `framework/rules/computations.md` selects durable input representations from the actual mathematical, verification and reproduction need.
  Temporary pipeline data remains replaceable output, and reliably reconstructible data may use sufficient retained generation materials and validation in place of a stored generated dataset when reconstruction cost is reasonable.
  Necessary irreconstructible or costly data and adopted objects requiring retention use selected tracked native input, preferring sufficient compact witnesses or certificates where appropriate.
  Retention choices and cleanup account for actual consumers, fixed evidence and review scope, with adequate preservation and affected checks preceding removal.
  `framework/rules/organization.md`, the computation action, J intake and personal transfer in `framework/rules/intake.md`, `framework/templates/mathematics/computation.md`, `mathematics/computations/readme.md` and `scripts/readme.md` use the same decision.
- **User manuals and instruction examples.** Update the six current manuals in `user-manual/` and their corresponding files in `user-manual/user-instructions/`, covering English, Chinese, French, German, Japanese and Spanish.
  The supplied updates cover startup and execution examples, writing and review, Git delivery, computation retention and material maintenance, with current localized links.
  Maintenance examples route external-literature defects to sufficient U/C/L evidence or a relevant U, and local defects or failed local dependencies to erratum; repair examples apply conformity-stage isolation only to conformity work, preserving ordinary literature preparation and actual mathematical-review duties.
- **Localized agent-harness guides.** Replace `user-manual/agent-harness.md` with `user-manual/harness/agent-harness.md` and update the manual links.
  Add the corresponding Chinese `agent运行环境.md`, French `Environnement d’exécution d’agents.md`, German `Agenten-Laufzeitumgebung.md`, Japanese `エージェント実行環境.md` and Spanish `Entorno de ejecución de agentes.md` in `user-manual/harness/`.
  The supplied guides describe agent execution environments and their setup and access references.

## Additional actions for this version

Ordinary synchronization through the [installed-framework upgrade procedure](../versioning.md#upgrading-an-installed-framework), including bounded merges of local edits and the documented guide replacement, is sufficient.
This version requires no historical-question backfill, stored-material conversion, configuration migration or host-run startup.

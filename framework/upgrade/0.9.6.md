# Upgrade from 0.9.5 to 0.9.6

The target is `0.9.6`.
The preceding official tag `0.9.5` identifies [commit 87b5860fdd4d483bc80c7942b63ad4126589b9e3](https://github.com/vtejdn/acme/commit/87b5860fdd4d483bc80c7942b63ad4126589b9e3).
This PATCH release clarifies manuscript work, source-evidence reuse, conversation retention and main-agent research decisions.
Existing 0.9.5 installations can continue using their materials and project setup after the [ordinary in-place upgrade](../versioning.md#upgrading-an-installed-framework), without stored-material migration or a removed tool interface.

## Changes

- Clarify the writing agent's drafting, revision and completion responsibilities, the boundary between current manuscript content and authorized writing choices, reader-oriented coherence and checks proportionate to actual changes in `framework/rules/writing.md`.
  Extend explicit logical connections to explanatory passages serving motivation, comparison, applicability or transition, and add a reader-perspective check for necessary missing relationships before delivery.
- Unify writing and proof review under one manuscript action in `framework/agent-actions.md`.
  Review only writing under the four writing layers when the request and any applicable review arrangement together leave the review type unspecified.
  Explicit requests can select proof review alone or both kinds of review.
  Writing review distinguishes required corrections from optional suggestions and uses `revisions.md` in each manuscript's own directory, including dedicated subdirectories for independent manuscripts that share a source directory.
  Proof findings use `review.md` or the existing adequate mathematical-review home, with links from needed revisions when both reviews are requested.
  Writing self-checks and the writing part of manuscript review use one common standard and procedure in `framework/rules/writing.md`.
  Corrections to manuscript text and citation records follow the actual editing authority.
  Independent initial reviews form findings from permitted inputs before consulting existing opinions.
  Later review, recheck, cleanup and author transfer select the actual manuscript and its own opinions.
  See the [revision-list rule](../rules/writing.md#manuscript-revisions).
  Revision requests use the selected writing opinions or mathematical-review evidence under their respective maintenance rules.
  Writing, review and author transfer carry forward attributable unresolved opinions from a prior single-manuscript list for the selected manuscript when it shares its source directory.
  Independent writing review bases qualification on participation in developing the reviewed exposition and structure and on inherited development context, separately from proof review.
  The rolling mathematical-review link in `framework/rules/proof-audits.md` points to the responsible manuscript-local rule.
- Make companion records conditional on their actual purpose and consolidate repeated guidance, keeping writing-choice precedence in `framework/rules/preferences.md`, writing responsibilities in `framework/rules/writing.md` and concise action entrances in `framework/agent-actions.md`.
- Align the manuscript actions and record navigation in `mathematics/manuscripts/readme.md`.
- Clarify source-error routing in `framework/rules/records.md`, `framework/rules/literature-audits.md` and the relevant summaries.
  Reuse adequate, locatable U/C/L evidence in place, including when reclassifying misplaced external-source items.
  Use U for distinct source findings that need their own record and keep unresolved defects in local materials in erratum with links to the applicable source evidence.
  The same rule applies at the related entrances in `framework/rules/organization.md`, `framework/rules/workflow.md`, `framework/agent-actions.md` and `framework/rules/proof-audits.md`.
  Search evidence, source preparation and dependency follow-up use the same reuse rule in `framework/rules/literature-search.md`, `framework/rules/literature.md` and `framework/rules/literature-audits.md`.
  New findings and changed use limits stay at their responsible homes, with affected consumer obligations checked at their actual scope.
- Apply the existing material-retention and closeout rules to substantive mathematics produced during repository questions and discussions.
  Complete the applicable preservation before delivering the substantive answer or handoff when the author and material scope are established and writing is authorized.
  When writing is restricted, report the precise finding, available evidence and scope still to be preserved.
  The closeout rule in `framework/rules/workflow.md` owns this duty, with an entrance in `framework/agent-actions.md`.
  See [Closing an Exploration Round](../rules/workflow.md#exploration-round-closeout).

- Strengthen main-agent responsibility for overall mathematical judgment in `framework/rules/workflow.md`, with a concise reference from `framework/agent-actions.md`.
  Within a coupled mechanism, constructions, parameter choices and witness requirements call for sustained work by a suitably capable researcher.
  Independent mechanisms can each retain a researcher for the whole construction and proceed in parallel as their dependencies, authority and resources permit.
- Extend investment checks in `framework/rules/strategies.md` to the decisive later gaps needed for the current allocation judgment.
  The same chain attempt can identify the first unsupported premise for actual assembly and continue conditionally to check later incompatibilities or losses that affect allocation.
  Explicit provisional premises keep the evidence scope visible.
  Early ideas retain proportionate conceptual exploration under the existing research authority.
- Connect candidate delivery in `framework/rules/agent-S.md` to the main agent's actual research assignments and feedback decisions.
  A selected candidate leads to an executable next investigation, whose evidence informs subsequent allocation in the existing responsible materials.
- Add proof and refutation direction reassessment to `framework/rules/strategies.md`, with references from the main-agent duties and agent-S exploration.
  Sustained substantive work that leaves the core gap and available mechanisms without meaningful progress calls for an actual allocation judgment, giving priority consideration to refutation of the original proposition.
  A selected reversal changes the principal research assignments and uses the proposition's exact negation to define the next bounded investigation.
  For a Strategy shared by several Tasks, a reversal changes the selected Task's association scope and allocation, while disposition and priority reflect the whole route.
  Subsequent changes follow new mathematical understanding, candidate mechanisms or actual conditions and the existing route-resumption rules.
  Task completion, effective execution limits and counterexample validation retain their responsible contracts.

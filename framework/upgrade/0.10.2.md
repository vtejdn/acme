  The agent fixes the actual source commit, follows relevant Task/S relationships and necessary mathematical inputs, chooses adequate receiving content and preserves exact source, authorship and evidence facts without per-file approval.
  Select needed versions and actual inputs rather than copying all family history by default; an explicit narrower or all-version instruction retains its scope in the selected view.

- Clarify `framework/rules/tasks.md` and `framework/material-format.md` so that a source Task used for collection is distinct from an installed local Task.
  A joint entrance follows relevant actual individual Task-to-S relationships; another human's ordinary Task/E authority, live execution, private settings and manuscript projects remain outside receipt.
  A necessary joint T contract keeps its existing narrow receiving contract, and material-only receipt starts no local research or E.

  Optional tools retain their explicit selected-unit and mechanical-protection contracts, without a Task crawler, required command, new plan fields, whole-library dependency graph or historical compatibility reader.

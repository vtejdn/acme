# Upgrade from 0.7.1 to 0.8.0

The preceding official release is tag `0.7.1` at commit `a18ffa71f48301ee783955d3d5256d226722a5cb`; the target is `0.8.0`.
Optional prose-processing interfaces, literature management, U identities and receiving-input responses change incompatibly during 0.x development, without obsolete readers or response aliases.
Use the [installed-framework upgrade procedure](../versioning.md#upgrading-an-installed-framework).

## What changed

- **Agent-owned prose.** The action guide, material format and relevant `framework/rules/` entries place body-reference selection and final text with the agent.
  Core links/paths/publication/Git-view/source-reference code, selected checks, intake and the affected `scripts/acme/materials/` readers/writers retain explicit operations and bounded declarations instead of automatic body conversion.
- **Literature and U.** Literature, audit, search and reuse rules, the source-unit template and literature/Task entrances use rolling author-owned U, concise source notes and selected original preparation.
  Local literature accounts and all literature fingerprints retire.
  `scripts/acme/checks/literature.py`, `scripts/acme/checks/literature_cli.py` and `scripts/check-literature-storage.py` are removed; `.gitignore`, intake/source helpers and `scripts/readme.md` follow the new contract.
- **Inputs and collaboration.** Acquired-revision input responses remove the input-row `scope`; actual scope stays in responsible prose and unchanged decision/review/acquisition fields.
  Locator-only cross-author maintenance and shared-repository work-start/integration rules retain their author, source, review and execution limits.
- **Human documents.** The six English, Chinese, French, German, Japanese and Spanish manuals under `user-manual/` update the current-version sentence, and `user-manual/publication-example.md` has a heading adjustment.

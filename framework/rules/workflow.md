# Research Workflow

Follow explicit human instructions and applicable agent instructions.
Apply the [core principles and interpretation](../readme.md#core-principles-and-interpretation) to every action and to conflicts, omissions, ambiguity and discretionary choices in this workflow.
Selectable research defaults follow [preference precedence](preferences.md#scope-and-adoption) within its permitted scope; repository settings and document choices do not by themselves waive evidence, preservation or authority boundaries.

## Agent and tool responsibilities

The agent reads the actual materials and decides content, work/edition identity, necessary homes and inputs, review applicability and authorization.
Reuse still-applicable judgments.
Tools optionally execute explicitly chosen objects, field updates, files and mappings, and report bytes, paths, differences, request/response facts and structural constraints.
At an actual public-reference operation, normalize and classify the final selected target before watching, reading its contents or checking an anchor, using the existing access rules and designated view.
For local navigation, use the existing single URL decoding and lexical components to reject protected targets and Windows trailing-dot/space aliases of source prefixes, including their necessary ancestors, before target resolution, reparse or existence checks.
Canonical logical source navigation supplies no source-file guard or availability condition, and ordinary preparation/publication does not resolve an unselected current or retired source root to classify another target.
Fixed Git intake and necessary acquired-record validation use the actual selected view throughout their consumers under the [intake rule](intake.md#scope-and-routing), without inspecting same-named sending worktree objects.
The agent chooses necessary inputs and canonical receiving arrangements; recorded local keys retain their owning repository/view, while actual local sources, Preparation and Batch publication keep their physical protections.
Apply the actual navigation, declared mathematical-input, concrete source-file or selected private-owner contract; a body link or native fragment alone supplies no stronger file-access or evidence obligation.
Keep a prohibited-target diagnostic, a missing necessary input and a stale selected fact distinct; do not expose private contents or automatically substitute another source, ancestor, input or decision to obtain success.
Tool success supplies no semantic equivalence, source adequacy, review sufficiency or permission.
For the actual operation, the agent chooses suitable available PDF reading, text extraction, page rendering, OCR and LaTeX compilation capabilities.
Reuse applicable host tools, project environments and build settings, confirming only what that operation needs without a compulsory inventory or fixed tool sequence.
If a dedicated capability is absent, choose an adequate general tool or actually available alternative source format after checking the relevant version and applicability; another format establishes only the features it exposes, not PDF layout.
Supplement needed tools only within actual authorization and environment constraints, without automatic installation, configuration or bootstrap.
When a capability limit leaves required work incomplete, tell the human author in the normal response or handoff which source passage, formula, page, build or check is affected, how that limits the claimed result and what useful action remains.
Retain necessary substantive limits at their existing responsible home, continue unaffected authorized work and distinguish unperformed inspection or compilation from actual results.
Apply the existing [progressive-reading](literature.md#progressive-reading), [local-build](writing.md#reproducible-local-builds) and [temporary-storage](organization.md#temporary-storage) duties; document build workspaces are not an alternate home for disposable literature text extraction or literature page images.
Check the result and necessary direct consumers; record required facts in their existing homes without a confirmation flag, receipt or compulsory command.
For an explicit J source refresh, the agent identifies the same original and selected current scope and maintains necessary retained inputs and actual consumers under the [intake rule](intake.md#homes-and-independent-reuse).
Copying selected files supplies no consumer or mathematical review, and earlier acquisition scopes do not select themselves as current dependencies.
Explicit source declarations and same-line fingerprints separate primary artifact facts from Resource/holding relationships; the agent prepares actual account changes and the tools check every supplied input before selected batch publication.
Typed namespace occupancy, fixed identity entrances, numerical/field constraints, OS locking, exclusive creation, stale-input refusal and rollback remain mechanical responsibilities.
Direct collaboration, same-human body maintenance, shared navigation/registration and the two kinds of actual conflict follow the [identity rule](identity.md#collaboration-maintenance-and-conflicts).
Apply its scoped mathematical-recheck approval only when that conflict actually arises; ordinary authorized research and maintenance keep their existing authority.

## Exploration, Registration, and Acceptance

Before direct creation/registration or retention of C/J/R/L/U, apply the [common content decision](records.md#substantive-material-retention) to necessary content and adequate existing homes, then the kind-specific obligations.
A draft is not a prerequisite, and an already adequate record needs no duplicate.
Apply obligations to the current action and intended use of each material.
Exploration, candidate registration and acceptance can occur together within one Task; they are not successive gates, a global Task mode, or new status fields.

| Action | Required scope and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Explore a question or test a mechanism | Identify the current question, attempted range, provisional assumptions and what the next test could distinguish. The final statement, hypotheses or quantifiers may still be developing. An authorized experiment or conditional derivation does not require a Claim ID or a completed mathematical review before it can begin or continue. |
| Register a candidate or organize a substantive reusable project statement | State the important proposition precisely, identify actual inputs and gaps, and register its material/version under the [Claim rules](claims.md#claim-registration). An unresolved candidate uses open; registration does not certify the result. |
| Accept a result as established, adopt a checked refutation or report Task completion | For proved/refuted assessments, check the complete supporting argument or refutation, applicability, resolved mathematical dependencies and applicable [review evidence](proof-audits.md) under the [assessment rules](claims.md#separate-assessment-fields). For a Task, apply its stated completion criteria to its actual deliverable. Existing accepted inputs and compatible inherited reviews retain their recorded scope. |

The following events determine when additional obligations apply:

| Trigger | Required action |
| --- | --- |
| Substantial proof investment, including a nontrivial intermediate step | Perform [knowledge reuse](knowledge-reuse.md#reuse-before-proof) before that investment. Routine direct calculations do not require a search for every line. Calling sustained proof work exploratory does not remove this trigger. |
| Recognition of a named or established result | Apply the independent [source-check trigger](knowledge-reuse.md#recognized-known-results) during exploration, completing or explicitly retaining the outstanding check before handoff. |
| A finding becomes useful, affects the research direction or must support continuation | Preserve a sufficient [exploration record](ledger.md#exploration-and-history). Routine scratch work without a substantive finding needs no separate operation log. Retained or cited computation evidence follows the [computation rules](computations.md). |
| A substantive project statement is organized for reuse, or an identified conjecture/obligation needs a canonical record | Apply Claim registration and record the actual status and dependencies. Routine specialization of an accepted source is governed by [knowledge reuse](knowledge-reuse.md#reuse-before-proof). |
| A result is treated as established, selected as reviewed support or used to close a Task | Satisfy the applicable acceptance, proof-selection and completion requirements before updating the responsible assessment or reporting that outcome. |

Always distinguish verified inputs, provisional assumptions, computational observations and checked arguments.
An unresolved hypothesis may be used to explore its consequences, provided the premise and resulting conditional scope stay explicit; it cannot silently become an established input to an unconditional conclusion.
Local checks are useful at any point and do not require completion of a whole review before further exploration.

These rules operate within the authorized Task or current instruction.
Existing execution limits, source-intake requirements and protected [main-theorem/objective decisions](claims.md#lemma-extraction-and-approval) retain their own triggers.
Do not introduce a stage-transition approval, a mandatory Claim for every experiment, or an additional log or directory merely to apply this section.

Continue arguments directly in D and continued/reusable programs in their responsible J program home.
Generated output uses that J's `mathematics/computations/outputs/<J>/` home under the [computation rule](computations.md#computation-output-location).
A failed or unreviewed attempt is still mathematical work, not a reason to place it in root temporary storage.
Keep useful findings and failures at their responsible homes; a handoff links those materials.
Small interactive trials need no new record.
The [temporary-storage boundary](organization.md#temporary-storage) permits operational isolation without creating a scratch-to-accepted stage.

## Proportionate Execution

Select the activity covered by the current instruction before reading or changing records: discussion, maintenance, Task setup, research advancement, or review.
Before starting or continuing affected work, read the current local [version metadata](../version.json) and entrances, then the current rules governing that activity, the relevant object and its directly needed inputs.
Reread the current needed entrances and governing rules before dependent operations when the installed framework version changes, an applicable rule's content, authority or scope changes, or the continued applicability of earlier actual reading cannot be established.
This includes an upgrade during an existing assignment and local rule edits before version advancement; an unchanged version number does not establish unchanged applicable text.
Old conversations, compressed summaries, persistent memory and recollection of an earlier framework version may locate relevant files but cannot replace the required current-file reading or establish that old instructions remain applicable.
Reuse only actual reading in the current context whose continued applicability is established; a remembered assertion that the rules were read is insufficient.

Reusing a daily public-version result, an unavailable result, disabled or offline network checks and delegation do not waive this current-rule duty.
Subagents read the current rules needed for their assigned activity even when reusing the responsible main agent's metadata result.
A newer official version that has not been installed does not replace the governing local rules or authorize treating remote instructions as current local authority.
After an authorized upgrade, apply the rereading duty before resuming affected work, preserving the assignment's actual authorization and Task/E stopped conditions.

Read only the assigned work's needed rules and direct inputs, without an unrelated full-framework or historical-library reread on every turn or tool call.
A new tool call alone is not a reason to reread the same rules or an unrelated historical example.
Use bounded searches/section reads.
If output is truncated, recover the missing applicable text and exceptions before acting; a partial output is not a completed read.
Do not add a last-read-version field, reading register, rule fingerprints, receipts, signatures or a script that certifies reading or understanding.
Current-file reading and judging the continued applicability of earlier actual reading remain agent responsibilities.

Keep each fact in its responsible record and update only necessary consumers.
Finish the affected edit/publication batch before maintaining its navigation and checking the final prepared bytes.
Do not check generated output while another command is still changing it.
Repeat a passed check when relevant bytes change, a failure is repaired, or a new risk justifies the additional scope; do not repeat it merely for another status update.
Working files, staged contents and committed trees are distinct; inspect the actual bytes needed for the requested operation under the [delivery rules](identity.md#submission-checks).

Normal success needs a concise result.
Expand diagnostic output for a failure, unexpected scope or a requested investigation.
Wait on an already running command instead of launching duplicates; use a suitable bounded waiting interval and provide meaningful progress when useful.
An unexplained delay calls for a targeted diagnosis, not repeated generation or a broad audit by default.

These are execution defaults, not a new reporting system.
Do not create per-read logs, permanent timing ledgers, validation receipts or another status registry to demonstrate compliance.
Keep substantive mathematical findings and actual review evidence in their existing homes.

## Registering and Activating a Task

Apply the [Task rules](tasks.md) and distinguish registration, authorization, review and execution:

| Instruction or situation | Normal result |
| --- | --- |
| Record a question awaiting authorization | proposed Task |
| Establish a clearly assigned research objective | active Task under the actual assignment |
| Continue an existing objective | Inspect its current contract and lifecycle; use the existing Task when it is the intended objective |
| Begin research as well as establish the objective | Complete setup, then apply the execution policy and any recorded stop/resumption conditions |

Check the relevant Task index and likely matching contracts to avoid accidental duplication.
Write one precise contract with the target, hypotheses and quantifiers, scope/conventions, completion criteria, actual authorization, and necessary starting references.
A descriptive anchor can be fixed before allocation; use the ID returned by the tool instead of predicting the next serial.
Optional template sections need content that helps continuation, not placeholders proving that setup occurred.

Register a clear assignment with its complete contract, actual human author and initial decision with date, proposed/active status, real authority/reason and exact contract relation.
The optional writer `python scripts/material.py create --kind T --file task.md` allocates and returns a canonical path, defaulting an unspecified new Task to A under the [Task rule](tasks.md#task-records).
An active assignment needs no invented setup review, second activation decision or execution run.

Activation does not require a separate contract-review document.
Record a genuine review when performed, with its actual scope; retain its concrete findings without a Task-wide review label.
A new completion still requires its applicable completion review.
Task setup alone does not require a Claim, strategy, audit file, mathematical-operation entry, maintenance report or independent reviewer.
Add related records only when the substantive work calls for them.

Update necessary links and generated navigation when the new objective affects them, then check the final affected changes.
Task setup creates no E and grants no execution, usage baseline or allowance; prepare or reuse the local boundary only for actually selected execution under the [execution rule](execution.md).
A joint coordination T has no E and is used through the actual human's individual Task.
Keep live run state out of the contract and the mathematical Task decision; use a stable link to the [execution rules](execution.md).
If the instruction also authorizes immediate research, continue under those rules without another setup approval.
An active Task does not resume a stopped run or bypass an applicable execution limit.

Begin or resume with read-only inspection.
Discussion, reading and maintenance analysis do not require generated-file writes.
Before research advancement inspect the relevant selected material decisions, contracts and execution state.
Routine startup does not trigger a full audit.
Choose any useful scoped material, link or storage checks for the actual question, and resolve relevant findings before relying on them.
A stale index does not change the authored decision.

Refresh navigation after the affected material/configuration batch has settled, when its view is needed.
The responsible agent may edit affected C/T/S navigation directly from the responsible materials and exact selected decisions, including populated generated rows, or use `python scripts/render-research-navigation.py` when useful.
Coordinate the affected write scope before editing and preserve unrelated and concurrent changes; reuse adequate navigation until relevant inputs change.
The optional renderer derives the indexes without a catalog authority or full proof/history audit, retaining its short lock, input rechecks and shared publication protections when selected.
For bounded discovery, use ordinary scoped search and reading or the optional `material.py discover`, `indexes` or `routes` commands with their stated scope.
Use operation-local material and reference readers for the actual question; completed authoring or rendering adds no aggregate delivery audit.
Run observations and execution queries do not trigger mathematical navigation regeneration.

The following sequence applies to mathematical research advancement; discussion, maintenance and Task setup use their applicable scope above.

1. Read the root README and the stable mathematics entrance, then verify the relevant objective in the [task index](../../mathematics/tasks/index.md).
   Check the working tree before editing and preserve existing work.
   A currently authorized maintenance or discussion request does not itself select a new research step.
   Before autonomous task advancement, read the actual operator's applicable personal execution policy, the current Task contract and its stable [E record](execution.md), including still-effective stopped conditions.
   Obtain required fresh readings at the policy's boundaries without retaining a quota diary or temporary response.
   Keep only the current exact Task reference, state and still-effective conditions in the stable E, with an incremental baseline/window only when actually applicable.
   Stop on the first stopping observation or monitoring failure and directly retain the effective stopped condition.
   An active Task does not itself resume a stopped run.
   For actual shared Git research, the responsible main agent completes the [round's collaboration update](tasks.md#shared-git-research) before new substantive work and reads its relevant changes before relying on the local entries below.
   Subagents reuse that actual same-round result; the responsibility applies even when adequate existing mathematics makes a new external literature search unnecessary.
2. Follow the relevant [Claim entries](../../mathematics/claims/index.md) and [strategy index](../../mathematics/strategies/index.md) within the authorized task.
   Read the linked arguments and audit units needed for the next step; do not treat historical checkpoints as active instructions or reread unrelated history by default.
3. Identify the current question, known and provisional assumptions, and smallest useful next step under the [work-obligation rules](#work-obligations).
   During exploration, state which parts of the eventual target remain to be determined.
   Prefer resolving a known gap or testing a concrete mechanism over starting an unrelated line of inquiry.
   Record the route's established or heuristic relationship to the task and its continuation or redirection conditions.
4. Before substantial proof work, apply [reuse before proof](knowledge-reuse.md#reuse-before-proof), including for intermediate steps.
   Read, derive, compute, or search for counterexamples as appropriate.
   Preserve substantive evidence in its responsible record, including unsuccessful approaches and their exact scope.
5. Update the responsible evidence and affected Claim entries; reconsider route priorities when appropriate and update task records/index when their authorized scope, selection or completion changes.
   Leave the relevant records sufficient for another agent to resume without relying on chat history.

When choosing routes or responding to an obstruction, search the relevant Claims, arguments, source audits, related strategies and failed attempts.
Explain consequential applicability, limitations and adaptations in the consuming route.
Cite fixed evidence for mathematical reliance; a rolling route or source analogy is not proof.
Useful lessons remain with the actual route or a substantive fixed synthesis, without a mandatory adoption form or reciprocal feedback obligation.
Receive prepared local content under the common [adaptation rule](records.md#receiving-and-adapting-selected-content), retaining exact necessary source scope and the original human.
Later corrections to another human's authored body belong in the correcting human's own material.

If no active research objective is recorded, make that absence explicit and continue only work covered by the current instruction, such as framework maintenance or an authorized source study.
A proposed question is not automatically an active task.
Within an authorized objective, agents may adjust methods and pursue justified routes without seeking repeated approval for ordinary research decisions.

Agents may autonomously add, revise, split, merge, reprioritize, pause, and restart strategies within an authorized research objective.
These are ordinary research decisions and do not require a separate user request or confirmation for each change.
Initial strategy and substrategy priorities may be freely adjusted after exploration starts as actual needs change, unless the user explicitly requires a fixed priority or sequence.
Follow the [strategy-management rules](strategies.md#autonomous-strategy-management): preserve stable IDs and research history, record reasons and successor relationships, and keep the route index and handoff current.
Apply predecessor updates only to local writable routes; a local successor of public source route records its precise source and carried obligations without changing the source disposition.
A priority-based pause may be reversed when priorities change; new mathematical evidence is not required merely to resume a paused route.
Strategy changes do not by themselves change the task's target, quantifiers, completion criteria, or authorization.

Proceed autonomously with routine reading, web searches, relevant literature downloads, local notes, derivations, reversible edits, and computations within the assigned scope.
The [search policy](literature-search.md#autonomous-literature-search) governs when to search and how to extend or stop a search.
Apply the [ACME provenance boundary](intake.md#scope-and-routing) to framework-produced material; external literature acquires no intake-approval requirement from its hosting platform.
Resolve ordinary methodological choices using the available evidence.
Seek clarification when an unresolved ambiguity materially changes the objective or requires authority beyond the task; continue independent work where possible.

Apply the [result-organization policy](claims.md#lemma-extraction-and-approval): internal extraction, decomposition and proof/dependency organization within an assigned Task are autonomous, without separate extraction approval or lemma labels.
Preserve the Task target, original arguments, precise assumptions and actual review obligations.

## Mathematical Standards

- When undertaking a proof of an identified proposition, state its hypotheses, quantifiers, notation and conclusion precisely.
  Exploration aimed at finding that proposition follows the [work-obligation rules](#work-obligations); it may start from a question and provisional scope.
  Separate the original question from any stronger or weaker candidate.
- Label an established reduction as necessary, sufficient, or equivalent, with justification.
  A proposed relationship may remain explicitly heuristic or conditional.
  A failed stronger sufficient condition does not refute the task; a proved reformulation alone does not resolve its remaining obligation.
- Make the mathematical setting explicit when relevant: the algebra or operator algebra, the state or trace and its properties, boundedness or operator domains, the notion of independence, and the topology or mode of convergence.
  Check normalization conventions before combining results from different sources.
- Verify applicability before using a result as an established input.
  During a conditional exploration, mark any unchecked premise or interface and carry that limitation through the conclusion.
  Record actual dependencies and enough detail to reconstruct each nontrivial inference; do not hide a missing argument behind phrases such as "clearly" or "by standard arguments."
- Distinguish algebraic identities, analytic arguments, asymptotic statements, and numerical evidence.
  Finite matrix experiments, truncated expansions, and agreement with examples do not establish a general theorem or a limit interchange.
- Actively test boundary cases, degenerate cases, and plausible counterexamples.
  Check whether the proposed conclusion survives the loss of each essential hypothesis.
- Never fabricate citations, theorem statements, calculations, proof checks, or novelty claims.
  Mark uncertainty explicitly.
  Failure to find a result in a bounded literature search does not establish that it is new.

<a id="exploration-round-closeout"></a>
### Closing an Exploration Round

At the end of each exploration round, the responsible main agent first applies the [common content decision](records.md#substantive-material-retention) and determines which completed findings require Claim registration or fixed retention under the applicable material rules, and ensures that the necessary material organization, evidence bindings and consumer updates are completed.
Preserve the necessary content and repair its consumers before removing redundant drafts.
A delegation restricted to D/J authoring leaves the main agent responsible for performing or explicitly delegating this fixed-material work after receiving the result.
Genuinely continuing work remains in D with its actual unresolved scope; any unfinished organization and the responsibility for completing it belong in the existing material or handoff.

Fixing material does not itself establish a result: record its actual mathematical and review status under the [registration and acceptance rules](#work-obligations).
This responsibility does not require a new material for every experiment, a separate closeout report or an additional approval gate.
Applicable execution stops take precedence; preserve available evidence and the necessary handoff under those rules rather than extending research or review to finish organization.

For human-authorized joint Task participation, the main agent assesses the retained progress for [timely collaborative delivery](tasks.md#shared-git-research) and completes a useful coherent commit/push under the [default joint-research permissions](identity.md#shared-research-git-authority-and-integration), honoring explicit human limits without seeking a separate delivery grant.
Major progress may warrant earlier sharing during the round; closeout is not a fixed-frequency commit obligation, a proof verdict or permission to bypass an effective stop.

Before ending a task, ensure the responsible records support continuation and give the user a concise update with:

- What changed and where the supporting records are located.
- What was checked, what remains conjectural, and any unresolved dependencies.
- Why an approach stopped or changed, if applicable.
- The next concrete step and any input or resource needed to proceed.

Do not mark a research objective complete merely because an attempt ended or a time budget expired.
Completion requires meeting its recorded criteria.
When progress stalls, preserve the obstruction, consider another justified approach, and checkpoint the work instead of repeating an unchanged attempt indefinitely.

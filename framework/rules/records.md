# Research Records and Publication

The responsible mathematical material owns its assertions, selections and dated decisions.
Routine naming, path, link and layout maintenance preserves mathematical meaning and needs no new mathematical version, review, operation entry or correspondence register.
Check actual affected references and retain coherent edits and Git history.
Substantive mathematics retains its version and review requirements.
Shared registration/navigation maintenance and actual conflicts follow the [collaboration boundary](identity.md#collaboration-maintenance-and-conflicts), preserving substantive mathematics, authorship and captured provenance.

The agent makes these content and revision judgments under the [agent/tool responsibility](workflow.md#agent-and-tool-responsibilities).
Optional writers and checks execute explicit inputs and structural constraints; they neither certify preservation of meaning nor assign a responsible home from prose.

## Substantive content before its destination

Before creating, registering, retaining or reorganizing mathematics or evidence, decide what the actual content needs and whether an adequate responsible home already exists.
Apply this to C, J, R, L, U and other substantive materials, including direct creation without D.
Then apply that kind's registration, version, reproduction, source and review obligations.
Choosing another letter cannot make routine process substantive, and mathematical novelty is not the only reason evidence must be retained.

Preserve the exact question and hypotheses, complete necessary arguments, useful failed constructions or counterexamples, native programs/data needed for reproduction or continuation, consequential dependency changes, source applicability and unresolved inputs, and actual review findings, relationships and limitations.
Preserve original mathematical versions where the proof/version rules require them.
Necessary T decisions, S choices, E authority/stop/adoption facts and acquired-source provenance stay at their responsible homes.
Under the [core principles](../readme.md#core-principles-and-interpretation), historical administration and authorization history have no blanket retention duty.
Shorten or remove what serves no necessary mathematical, evidentiary, reproduction or current execution purpose, keeping effective authority and limits clear and affected consumers coherent.
Compatibility, old formats or old references alone do not require keeping that history or its obsolete container.

| Content and actual role | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Necessary mathematics/evidence missing from its responsible home | Retain it completely at the appropriate home, reusing or revising an existing material when adequate. Allocate a new identity only when needed. |
| Content already adequately retained | Link the actual fixed material and update consumers. Remove redundant setup or process without creating a replacement account, redirect or move receipt. |
| Useful failed or partial finding | Retain its hypotheses/range, decisive reasoning/evidence, exact failure or uncertainty and what remains possible. Failure of a method does not refute the goal. |
| Continuing investigation or needed unincorporated program/data | Keep its working D/J home and unresolved scope. Finishing another part does not authorize discarding it. |
| Routine allocation, moving, scheduling, progress or authorization chronology | Omit it during the relevant cleanup when it supplies no necessary mathematical, evidentiary, reproduction or current execution fact. Coherent edits and Git history suffice. |

C may register an actual conjecture or obligation with unresolved status; it is not a shell for a retired setup log.
J preserves needed native work with honest run status, including useful failure or not-yet-run continuing work, without an immutable account for every trial.
R records an actually performed review of fixed inputs, including partial or negative findings; reviewing an existing theorem can add necessary evidence.
An assignment, announcement or link check is not such a review.
U owns inspected source definitions/results/mechanisms, edition/passages, applicability and inspection limits; acquisition-only facts stay in bibliography/registry/intake.
L owns substantive exploration, comparison, synthesis, useful failure and limitations; formal project reviews of every actual agent relationship belong to R.
An ordinary local verification may stay with its argument or discussion without becoming a separate formal review.
Required registration and evidence duties remain even when the theorem appears elsewhere.

For the bounded action, read the candidate, applicable obligations, existing homes and actual consumers.
Identify missing necessary content before selecting a destination.
Preserve it at its actual mathematical and review scope, then update links, source maps, program inputs and substantive prose references.
Trace each use of a reused D address to its actual destination; do not use global token replacement or a permanent old-number map.
A setup-only draft whose mathematics is already fixed may disappear without a successor.
A mixed draft may be reduced only after preserving its unique necessary content; genuinely continuing work remains.

Inspect both **sufficiency** (no necessary mathematics, evidence, qualification or authority lost) and **necessity** (no new fixed account or paragraph kept solely for routine process, no duplicate adequate account).
Whole-container copying and passing parsers/links establish neither.
The role of a range, date or SHA sentence determines retention: delete purely administrative checking/digest history entirely, but retain a genuine result-domain or review limitation without requiring its old process wording.
Do not replace retired hashes with invented inspection history.

Meaning-preserving wrapper, title-depth and relative-link maintenance can remain in place only while the actual argument, targets, hypotheses, decisions, verdicts and provenance keep their meaning.
A changed mathematical argument or dependency follows revision/adoption rules; later changes to authored bodies follow the same-human and separate-correction boundary below.
Run checks relevant to changed behavior after inspecting content.
No per-paragraph classification sheet, preservation hash, new registry, closeout report or additional approval is required.

## Receiving and adapting selected content

Apply the common content decision to both external intake and a human's own [personal import](intake.md#personal-import).
Read the selected mathematical unit, necessary inputs and adequate receiving candidates before choosing local content, references or deduplication.
Preserve exact questions, hypotheses, quantifiers, domains, definitions, conventions, conclusions, complete necessary arguments and consequential dependencies.
Retain useful alternate proofs, counterexamples, failures, native inputs and actual source/review scope, attribution, uncertainty and unresolved obligations.
Adapt prose, classification, layout, numbering and references to current receiving rules without byte equality or a whole-source-container requirement.
Confirmed sufficient local content is reused by exact reference; partial duplication retains its unique part, and unresolved equivalence remains unresolved.
One author's units may be split or combined with complete necessary per-unit source correspondence; a synthesis of different authors belongs to its actual single synthesizing human with citations.
An available older source can be adapted without changing its repository or fabricating an assessment, review, argument or missing evidence.
When a helper reads older material, keep the necessary source identity and Task-family facts separate from validation of the adapted receiving result.
Obsolete source layout or administrative fields alone do not require a source upgrade or refusal of an otherwise adequate preparation.
The agent judges preservation, correspondence, need and review applicability, writes the receiving content and required source facts, and inspects the actual result and affected references.
Ordinary reading, editing, copying and existing registration/allocation mechanisms suffice; a dedicated command, prepared-text payload, mapping plan or uniform batch protocol is not a receiving prerequisite.
Optional helpers apply their payload, mapping and publication contracts only to callers choosing those interfaces; structural validation establishes no mathematical preservation or review-applicability verdict.

Every authored mathematical document has exactly one human author.
Initial authorized intake permits the preparation above while retaining the original human and source assessments truthfully.
After receipt, another human explains a gap, correction, replacement argument, computation or synthesis in that human's own suitable document with exact references.
Calling a body edit administrative does not permit changing another human's authored document.
Only the established same human may directly revise it under actual authority, fixed-version and review rules.
For different local/external identity representations, use explicit adopted correspondence at the existing identity/intake home; matching names or a caller-selected author supply no permission.
Shared registration and derived navigation retain their own maintenance duties.

## Existing and newly made mathematical decisions

The agent judges from actual content whether preparation preserves an existing decision or makes a new one.
Preserve genuine decisions' actual assessment/lifecycle, date precision, adopted evidence and review limits when applicable.
A new file, anchor, physical version or absent Git preimage alone establishes no newly made mathematical decision or acceptance.
Operation-local readers validate their chosen field syntax, identities, exact references and availability in the actual view, without inferring new dates or retrospective review bindings from differences.
No copied-decision marker, import category, historical classifier, waiver, permanent mapping or preservation receipt is needed.
A genuinely new decision uses its actual UTC minute and applicable present acceptance/review obligations, even when its mathematics is older.
The optional explicit new-decision checks validate only the agent's chosen actual new scope; preserving an unsupported old assertion does not make it accepted.
Changing mathematical obligations or evidence applicability retains its actual revision and review requirements.

## Identity and versions

Local fixed C/T/R/L files use `Author_Kind_F0001_v1.md`; rolling S/D use `Author_Kind_F0001.md`.
A J computation has one numbered directory whose README and native children share its identity.
Read the kind, line, number and physical version from the path and the title from its main heading.
The short author header records the registered human and applicable original framework/source provenance.
See the [material format](../material-format.md).

Choose a research line letter explicitly or inherit only that letter from an actual Task.
Each author/origin/kind/line has its own numeric sequence: F0001 through F9999, F10000 and onward.
Never recycle a fixed C/T/R/L/U/J identity or continuing S identity, or switch to another letter because four digits are exhausted.
The [allocation state](identity.md#allocation-and-concurrency) preserves the next unissued C/T/R/L/J or continuing S family number even after its highest surviving material is removed.
A D address remains stable during use and becomes reusable only after its necessary content is adequately retained at responsible homes, actual consumers are updated and the redundant draft is retired.
Already retained mathematics and setup-only residue require no new fixed successor.
Reuse carries no earlier draft authority.
A materially different statement or question gets an appropriate new C/T family; another proof of the unchanged statement stays with that family.

Physical v1, v2 and onward identify actual fixed mathematical material.
A substantive revision retains the old file and creates the next version.
An unchanged-mathematics assessment, proof selection or lifecycle decision is appended within the responsible C/T material; it does not create a metadata-only version.
Retained fixed decisions remain at their exact anchors, preserving actual mathematical and evidence selections and currently effective authority.
Purely administrative or authorization history may be reduced under the [common content decision](#substantive-material-retention), without a new mathematical version or alteration of necessary mathematics and evidence.
The latest numeric main material supplies the family entrance, whose explicit Current selector may choose an older decision.
File timestamps, an index row and an alternative proof do not make that choice.

Fixed U materials use `mathematics/audits/literature/SourceKey/Author_U001_v1.md`, retaining the existing source's unit sequence, source edition, exact passage and actual inspection limits.
This is separate from line allocation and from an external work's edition.
Definitions retain their own naming conventions.

An acquired original retains exact original authorship, source family/version, path, selected scope and registered repository correspondence in its responsible provenance.
C/T/R/L/J filenames use receiving authorN/repoN and an explicitly chosen local family, line, kind and physical version appropriate to the prepared content.
Those local identities need not equal the source's classification, line or version.
Headers bind the receiving author and origin namespace; a single-origin document also binds its exact source_material.
For a same-author combination with several original documents, retain their distinct per-unit source/local locators in the existing intake accounts rather than asserting one inaccurate source_material.
For U, that original path retains the original source key and unit number even when recipient bibliography-key/unit placement differs; the local anchor follows the recipient position.
Retain the unchanged original human, actual input version and exact correspondence in the existing intake operation, separately from the receiving content's version.
The immediate transport repository and a matching display name do not substitute for that identity.
Use explicit adopted external correspondences; names, addresses, foreign numbers and revisions alone establish no identity.
Do not replace the original identity or rewrite captured provenance to impersonate local authorship.
Establish actual human identity correspondence rather than inferring it from display names.

Research paths reject protected directory names `.git`, `.codex` and `.agents` without regard to case, unsafe traversal and undeclared symbolic links or junctions, including hidden/excluded locations.
The resolved path must remain outside protected metadata and the physical PDF store.
The declared `mathematics/literature/pdf/` source mount is read-only source access, not mathematical publication or intake storage.
Approved intake uses its explicit fixed source/file scope, not arbitrary recursive discovery.

## Acquired inputs and local revisions

An established same human may revise acquired C/T/R/L under ordinary authority, fixed-version, decision and review requirements while retaining the actual external identity and source header.
The agent names the actual fixed receiving input and adopted scope in the physical C/T/R/L revision's own ordinary Inputs section under the [material format](../material-format.md#acquired-receiving-inputs-in-local-revisions).
Those provenance inputs are independent of current decision selection; a decision's support/dependency relations retain their separate mathematical adoption and review meaning.
Its actual source_repository and source_material agree exactly with the retained revision header; an arbitrary earlier member of the family or another original source version cannot substitute for that input.
Keep the current local revision distinct from that acquired input and its original source facts; local additions do not become content or reviewed evidence of the earlier source.
The existing intake operation continues to describe the version actually acquired, without a new acquisition event, another local.path entry or an edit to the old outcome for the local revision.
Preserve the fixed input and its original operation unchanged.
Readers use the explicitly designated input and scope, rather than arbitrary body links, family membership, adjacent numbering, earliest/latest surviving versions or text similarity.
A missing or contradictory actual input remains a concrete issue to resolve in the responsible material, without automatic ancestry discovery.
For onward selection that includes local additions, distinguish the earlier acquired input from the actual revising repository, fixed local revision and scope supplying those additions in the existing source/local accounts.
These ordinary input relationships create no revision registry, copy marker, import payload or requirement to use a particular helper.
In an R revision, acquisition facts remain about its explicitly designated acquired input, while a genuine locally performed finding owns its current fixed locator, checking relationship, exact reviewed targets and limits.
Consume that local finding under the applicable new-review and acceptance requirements without requiring its anchor in the old intake operation.
Preserve inherited findings at their actual source scopes; neither the retained external identity nor the common R container transfers an earlier relationship or verdict to the local finding.
An unchanged retained finding and its fixed input keep their already-supported declaration syntax, including when the revision expresses the same targets as separate current-format rows.
Apply current declaration requirements to genuinely new or changed findings at their actual scope, without requiring a rewrite of the fixed input.
The actual author, mathematical revision, review scope and evidence obligations continue to apply.

## Time and human attribution

New C/T decisions, substantive S decisions or recorded updates, and operator, approval, intake and correction acts use their actual UTC minute timestamp under the [new-event rule](workflow.md#new-record-timestamps).
A genuinely added C/T decision requires `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM`, including when it assesses older mathematics; a year, month or date alone, a value with `Z`, an offset, seconds or fractions is invalid for it.
A timestamp is neither an identity nor a mathematical version.
Keep genuinely known year/month/date facts at their calendar precision for older mathematics and reviews, including historical reading or genuine reselection of an existing C/T decision; never invent a creation time, transition date or reviewer to fill a field.
Existing necessary full timestamps are normalized to UTC minutes under the maintenance handbook rather than retained in old formats.
Routine path, heading, link or layout maintenance creates no event or historical timestamp-backfill obligation.
Later contributions retain their own attribution.
Human registration does not register agents or models; review arrangement records what actually occurred.

| `repository/config.json`, `repository/people.json` | Own name/settings, compact `material_next` family-allocation state, readable local human names and actual Git/GitHub mappings; no timezone, registration timestamp, self number or shared active-user setting |
| `repository/repositories.json`, `repository/intake-people.json` | External repoN/authorN, qualified source facts and explicit two-way associations |
| Git `config.worktree`: `research.actor` / `research.actorWorktree` | Local bound operator for this checkout; not committed |
| `mathematics/claims/` and `mathematics/tasks/` | Exact statements/contracts, fixed decisions and explicit current selections, with their real authority and evidence |
| Fixed C, R, L, U and J materials in their responsible homes | C statements and substantial arguments; R formal agent reviews of all relationships; L exploration and synthesis; U source inspection; J computations with exact scope and group identity |
| Rolling S/D materials | Routes and continuing draft mathematics; meaningful earlier findings and received originals retain their preservation boundaries |
| `intake/<initiator>/*.json` | Actual operation time, recorder, instruction, original/transport provenance, scope and outcome |
| `execution/Author_E_SERIAL.md` | One current execution boundary per ordinary local Task family in every lifecycle state, with exact Task decision and effective conditions; a joint T has no E |
| Claim, Task and strategy indexes | Rebuildable navigation derived from authored materials and actual acquisition provenance |

C decisions own mathematical status, exact evidence selections and optional role; T decisions own lifecycle, authority, reason and completion evidence.
R findings own their actual agent checking relationship and scope, without a C/T aggregate review-status.
A C/T decision selects the exact statement/contract, argument, support, dependencies and review that it adopts.
Neither a generated table nor another file's PASS text selects acceptance.

Source assertion, actual acquisition and the recipient's mathematical use are separate facts.
Keep the first in original materials, the second in concise intake metadata and the third in the responsible consumer.
See [intake](intake.md).

Keep earlier mathematical statements, useful failed arguments, selected proofs, dependencies and necessary review evidence, even without a current consumer.
Lifecycle and authorization history follow the [common content decision](#substantive-material-retention); their category alone creates no permanent-retention duty.
The [review-retirement exception](proof-audits.md#review-retirement) permits removing a fully redundant old review container only after sufficient actual replacement and truthful retention and reconciliation of its necessary content, inputs and historical adoption facts.
Absence of current consumers or a later PASS alone does not permit that exception, and retirement never makes its fixed identity available again.
Historical gaps remain gaps: do not retroactively attach later reviews or invent missing transitions.
Remove obsolete purely administrative references; necessary mathematical or review evidence remains at its responsible home.

Remove persistent mathematical digests, including historical values and their writers/readers, for authored mathematics, review content, J inputs/programs/outputs and manuscript sources/builds/PDFs.
Preserve the actual reviewed inputs, verdict, relationship, scope, limits and useful failures through canonical paths, physical versions, anchors and available source revisions.
Literature-file fingerprints computed from actual local bytes remain at the sole source registry for accidental-damage checking; authored-material intake carries no literature baseline.
Transient input-change comparisons may be used without a saved mathematical digest.
No hash-history log, replacement manifest or mathematical byte gate is introduced.
The retired C/T JSON records, snapshots and discovery products are not a second reader or authoring API.

For locator-only maintenance, inspect all affected references and preserve the selected material, physical version, original identity, mathematical content, actual scope and review conclusions.
[Coordinated citation-key correction](literature.md#coordinated-key-renaming) is local source-owner maintenance for the same unchanged work and edition, with the ordinary old/new note and corrected consumers.
It does not permit a recipient to rewrite an imported original's captured key, path or original source binding; later acquisition of a corrected source binding has its own scope.
Neither maintenance nor a mere byte difference requires a new mathematical review.

## Internal storage boundary

The `repository/` entrance and every internal directory/file, execution storage and lock must be ordinary local filesystem entries.
Refuse junctions, symbolic links and other reparse points even if their targets lie inside the repository.
Inspect before initialization, locking and mutation, and recheck actual reads, writes, replacement and rollback.
A previously constructed Repository object is not a permanent storage clearance.

A boundary failure stops the operation.
Restore ordinary storage from verified local data; never follow a link to repair or delete its target automatically.
The separately declared literature PDF source mount is the only external-storage exception and cannot store publication or intake evidence.

## Execution metadata and mathematical versions

The [execution rules](execution.md) define one stable Markdown E per ordinary individual Task; a joint coordination T has no E.
E contains only its current exact relevant Task decision, execution state and still-effective conditions, without observations or history.
Task setup creates its minimal non-running E, completed Tasks retain it and every physical Task version may retain one unanchored operational link.
Other mathematical references to E or administrative records are disallowed.
Its exact Task decision links do not constitute proof, review or mathematical acceptance.
Task revision does not change the E address or resume a stop.
Custom overrides state that the agent evaluates their actual conditions; a structural check does not control the account or platform.

## Selected support and review binding

Use the [decision tables](../material-format.md#claim-and-task-decisions) only in their designated sections.
Relations are `statement`, `contract`, `argument`, `support`, `dependency` and `review`, each with an exact locator and meaningful applicability scope.
A selected Claim statement or Task contract belongs to the same receiving material family and original registered source binding.
A proofless open Claim needs no invented argument.
Ordinary prose links, source attribution, extraction ancestry and comparisons are not automatically logical dependencies.

A Claim argument relation selects the actual reasoning in the same or another applicable fixed C material.
L cannot supply that argument under an alternative support/dependency label.
Fixed support and dependency relations also exclude rolling S and mutable D.
Keep route associations as navigation and select the actual fixed mathematics.
Native J inputs inherit the group's header and identity.
U support records exact source edition/passage/inspection; it is not a C/T argument, an R review or a lifecycle decision.
A review's separate role never removes mathematical support that the argument actually needs.

A newly accepted Claim selects applicable fixed R findings covering its exact statement and argument.
Every formal project-review finding belongs to fixed R, including actual agent self-review, independent review and genuine older/source unknown relationships, with partial, negative, unresolved and unadopted findings preserved at their actual scope.
Independent acceptance requires applicable independent R coverage, and placement in R alone does not establish independence.
A newly completed Task selects a review covering its exact contract and every declared support/dependency.
The review material's [Review bindings](../material-format.md#review-bindings-for-new-accepted-decisions) identifies each covered input and actual scope.
Each R finding actually selected for a genuinely new acceptance also has its own explicit allowed relationship row; omission is not an explicit historical/source unknown declaration.
Newly authored or substantively revised findings declare their actual relationships at that finding scope, without backfilling unchanged older findings merely because they share a file.
Coverage of an older proof does not cover a changed one.
Existing historical findings remain at their recorded limits; reading or reselecting them does not fabricate a new review.

Reviewers check the complete argument and hypotheses at dependency interfaces, including unresolved or cyclic reliance.
Structural validation checks declared scope and locators, not mathematical sufficiency or every omitted dependency.
An empty relation list does not prove independence.
Accepted external results retain their actual inspection limits without requiring recursive proof audits.
The selected consumer explicitly decides whether to adopt a successor; old exact references never silently resolve to newer evidence.

## Mathematical review and changes

Preserve applicable original framework/source attribution through acquisition and onward transfer.
An inherited review is inherited evidence, not a newly performed independent review.
Source Task completion grants no local execution authority.
Non-ACME literature remains external-source evidence under the source-audit rules.
A receiver records its own judgments and feedback at its own material, rather than changing the original author's findings.

Mathematical changes to a fixed file create the next material version and an actual assessment at that scope.
A candidate proof remains a draft until the required review; an unresolved condition remains explicit.
An unaffected old proof may still support the unchanged Claim.
A changed completed Task contract requires explicit reopening or a new applicable completion decision.
Unchanged-mathematics decisions append without a physical version bump.
Preserve the actual self-review/independent-review relationship; a command does not perform a proof check.

Apply the [result-organization policy](claims.md#lemma-extraction-and-approval): internal Claim organization and proof repair within an assigned scope need no additional approval.
Main-theorem designation, objective changes and execution retain their actual authorization boundaries.
Maintain all affected direct consumers and meaningful history, then derive navigation.
Harmless shared-material maintenance causes no publication cascade.

## Commands

Prepare complete Markdown with the actual author and decisions.
Examples do not allocate identities or authorize intake:

```text
python scripts/material.py create --kind C --line F --file claim-input.md
python scripts/material.py create --kind T --line F --file task-input.md
python scripts/material.py show mathematics/claims/Author_C_F0001_v1.md
python scripts/material.py revise mathematics/claims/Author_C_F0001_v1.md --expect mathematics/claims/Author_C_F0001_v1.md#decision-initial --file revised-claim.md
python scripts/material.py append-decision mathematics/tasks/Author_T_F0001_v1.md --expect mathematics/tasks/Author_T_F0001_v1.md#decision-initial --file next-decision.md
python scripts/material.py select mathematics/claims/Author_C_F0001_v1.md --expect mathematics/claims/Author_C_F0001_v1.md#decision-initial --decision mathematics/claims/Author_C_F0001_v1.md#decision-reviewed
python scripts/render-research-navigation.py
```

The allocator returns the actual next unissued family address under the [allocation state](identity.md#allocation-and-concurrency); D keeps its separate reusable-address rule.
C/T revise, append and select check exact `--expect PATH#decision` under the existing local lock and reject stale choices.
These commands edit only the latest local main material under established same-human authorship and actual authority, preserving its author.
Substantive contributions use the responsible human's own numbered materials under the identity rule; tool capability does not authorize rewriting another human's mathematics.
Imported originals and fixed evidence retain their separate protection.
`research.py` supplies initialization and human identity operations; it is not a parallel C/T producer.

Correct evidence and affected material decisions first, render the three derived indexes, then perform the checks needed for the actual changed material or explicitly requested view.
Rendering plans before installing and writes only changed outputs, preserving no-op bytes/mtimes.
It neither creates a catalog authority nor audits mathematical truth.
Direct index edits are overwritten and cannot change acceptance.
Writes are atomic per file, not a distributed or multi-file transaction.

## Interrupted local publication

Inspect an interrupted operation's actual files, current selection and references before retrying.
Do not repeat a successful create/revise merely to recover its output.
Preserve unrelated working and staged changes.
The OS lock is released on process exit; persistent `repository/.lock` is normal.
Repair coherent sources, regenerate only needed navigation and run the scoped check.
Direct intake records the actual fixed source, selected scope and result once in `intake/<initiator>/`; it does not maintain proposal/approval phases or a second adoption register.

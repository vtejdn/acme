# Mathematical Operations and Exploration

Routine naming, path, link and layout maintenance preserves mathematical meaning and normally leaves only coherent edits and Git history.
It requires no new mathematical version, operation entry, standalone maintenance report or duplicate correspondence artifact.
Keep the responsible material and its explicit current selection coherent in place; substantive mathematics retains its version and review requirements.

Use the [agent/tool responsibility](workflow.md#agent-and-tool-responsibilities) for content retention, mathematical effects and necessary homes.
Optional structural checks and ordinary diffs do not make those judgments.
Important independently reusable propositions and substantial complete arguments belong in adequate C materials; L retains exploration, comparison, synthesis, meaningful failure and unresolved directions.
Formal project review findings belong in R for every actual agent relationship; ordinary checks needed within an argument or discussion need no separate R.
Decide ownership from actual content and use, without a proof-length threshold, title classifier or automatic extraction.

## Which Mathematical Operations Must Be Recorded

Apply the [common content decision](records.md#substantive-material-retention) before choosing L or another home.
The cases below describe L-related mathematical effects and their responsible records, rather than a separate cross-kind retention threshold.
Record an operation when it has a substantive mathematical effect or finding.
Use the following cases to determine the responsible record, not as a requirement to create a separate file for every row.

| Operation or finding | Required durable record |
| --- | --- |
| Introduce, materially change, generalize, weaken, split or merge a registered statement or one being organized for reuse | Exact before/after hypotheses and conclusion, mathematical reason and supporting argument or gap; retain or allocate Claim IDs under the identity rules and identify affected consumers. Tentative exploratory statements follow the [exploration conventions](#exploration-and-history) |
| Introduce a substantive new definition or construction, change its meaning, reconcile conventions with mathematical consequences, or establish an equivalence of constructions | Exact definition and source relationship, before/after content where applicable, supporting source result or project argument, and affected claims/proof versions; preserve earlier adopted definitions. Mere naming, parameter substitution or index maintenance is not by itself such a change |
| Extract shared lemmas or substantially reorganize a proof into reusable parts | Extracted statements, original proof sections, actual dependency changes, consumer interfaces and preserved originals; actual review links; internal organization within an assigned Task needs no separate extraction approval |
| Create an alternative proof, assemble a proof, select a new current version, revert or abandon that selection | Actual proof/version artifact or exact failed step; old/new version map, reason, review scope and outcome, preserving an unaffected earlier proof |
| Change substantive proof inputs or their required interfaces | The input and version used, the exact conclusion needed, hypothesis matching, replaced or unresolved obligations, and downstream effects; source review stays in its source audit |
| Assign or change a mathematical Role or select a main theorem | The affected statement, mathematical/expository reason, previous and resulting role, and the authorization required by the existing rule; a role label does not establish a proof |
| Establish a useful reduction, proof mechanism, boundary case, obstruction or failed implication | Precise scope, decisive reasoning/evidence, consequence for the claim or route, and remaining obligation; an exploratory finding need not immediately receive a Claim ID |
| Discover a mathematical defect, counterexample or substantive change in validity/review assessment | The challenged implication, affected scope and proof versions, correction or unresolved gap, review evidence and downstream reassessment; an unverified objection is recorded as such |

Ordinary use of an existing lemma in a new proof is recorded in that proof's dependency list; it needs no additional operation log unless the mathematical structure or an existing dependency interface is substantively changed.
A review finding or an attempt already recorded adequately in its responsible claim, audit or exploration section does not need a duplicate narrative.
Link those records when a shared reorganization needs to account for their consequences.

Routine reading without a substantive finding, tool calls, scratch calculations, typo/format changes that preserve meaning, file sorting, simple link repair, and ordinary repeated builds or checks do not require separate mathematical-operation records.
Keep computation or build evidence where the existing rules require it.
File relocation alone is not a mathematical operation: preserve links and historical review meaning under the existing maintenance rules; a maintained path alone is not an exact historical byte archive.
If a move accompanies a changed authoritative statement, proof selection or dependency structure, record that mathematical change and include the old/new paths as supporting metadata.

### Record Content and Timing

For a substantive operation, preserve the date, responsible human and actual authoring/checking arrangement, question/reason, mathematical before/after state or exact attempted step, evidence, outcome and unresolved consequences.
Link affected Claim IDs, proof versions, consumers and reviews when they actually exist or apply.
An exploratory attempt can refer to its authorized question, Task or route without inventing a Claim, proof version or completed review.
Add the existing user instruction or approval and its scope when the operation requires it.
Record whether a proposal was actually implemented; preparing a new proof does not itself switch the current one.
Distinguish extraction provenance, actual proof dependencies and expository relationships under the claim rules.

Record significant findings as they become usable or change the research direction, and record implemented changes before reporting them as adopted.
A single coherent batch can own several related operations.
For an approval-requiring action, prepare the concrete proposal first and record the decision when received; the existing approval rule is unchanged.
A record of an operation is not approval to perform it, and ordinary record keeping introduces no additional approval gate.

Within a research-operation record, keep a concise dated history of substantive decisions/outcomes separate from any mutable current version map.
Later corrections, reversals or scope changes should have a new dated entry explaining their effect; do not rewrite an earlier decision to suggest that it always had the new outcome.
Group routine implementation edits with the operation they implement.
Preserve unsuccessful reorganizations when they reveal a mathematical obstruction or explain why the old proof remains selected.

Use an existing claim's review/correction section for a small local change.
Create a dedicated research-operation record when a cross-claim extraction, shared proof assembly or several version transitions would otherwise scatter one mathematical decision across unrelated records.
Keep one coherent owner for that account and link it from the affected proofs and claim entries.
A new proof of the same statement gets a proof-version label, not automatically a new Claim ID.

## Exploration and History

The [work-obligation rules](workflow.md#work-obligations) govern when an attempt needs a durable record.
For a useful finding or handoff, retain the investigated question and scope, known/provisional assumptions, decisive method and evidence, actual outcome and uncertainty, and next action.
This may be a compact section in the existing responsible record.
Use the questions below as a content guide, not a mandatory standalone form for each calculation; omit inapplicable fields and retain the uncertainty where the target is still developing.

Use fixed `Author_L_F0001_v1.md` materials in mathematics/ledger/ for substantive attempts and preserved assessments, with the actual research line and dates in the body.
Create one through ordinary authorized authoring or the optional `material.py create --kind L --line F --file note.md` writer; later substantive revisions retain the previous physical version.
Either path maintains the existing material_next bound and necessary short-lock and exclusive-publication protections for fixed-family allocation under the [allocation rule](identity.md#allocation-and-concurrency).
All L materials, including source-specific reading supplements and cross-source syntheses, use this location and the same author/line L sequence.
Link the relevant source-audit home when applicable; source-local U records retain their own identities and inspection scope.
Dates record chronology; material identities preserve the substantive account.

Retain the program, necessary inputs, decisive parameters, relevant environment and a usable command plus mathematical checks.
Generated outputs may be regenerated at the same path, overwritten or removed when their actual consumers remain coherent.
Retain a particular witness, certificate or input when it is necessary to reproduce or verify the mathematical result.
Keep needed historical inspection facts in a concise summary under the [computation rule](computations.md), separate from outputs and logs; a long J report may be condensed around those facts and its useful mathematics.
Apply the [common content and maintenance boundary](records.md#substantive-material-retention): valid J edits and coherent output cleanup add no generic change reminder, acknowledgment or summary preservation gate; the agent retains needed facts and inspects relevant changes.
Separately fixed mathematical reports and dated proof reviews keep their conclusions and actual scope.
No per-run version, archive, fingerprint gate or classification registry is required.
Record failed or incomplete runs truthfully, and publish canonical output only after the claimed calculation completes.

An attempt record should answer:

- Which goal, route, or claim was investigated, under which assumptions?
- What method or discriminating test was attempted, and what evidence resulted?
- What was observed, conditionally derived or established, what remains uncertain, and where is the supporting calculation or argument?
- If the attempt failed, which assertion and parameter scope failed, and what remains possible?
- What changed relative to the previous attempt, and what is the next useful action?

Move Claim arguments into their responsible fixed C materials and link them from the chronological record.
Do not keep independent, diverging copies of a proof in a session log and a module.
During [same-author transfer](intake.md#personal-import), suspected L overlap with C, R, J or another kind follows [subagent comparison and responsible-home selection](records.md#same-author-transfer-and-duplicate-comparison), preserving unique exploration, useful failures, analysis and actual limits before trimming duplication.
Preserved original complete proofs and explicitly distinguished new versions follow the [proof-version rules](claims.md#proof-versions); their different historical or current roles must be visible.

When a literature search materially affects an attempt, route, or novelty assessment, include a compact account using the [search evidence fields](literature-search.md#search-evidence): the question and trigger, date and search scope, decisive sources and findings, remaining uncertainty, and reason for stopping or expanding.
Link source-specific checks in [literature audits](literature-audits.md) and acquisition metadata in the [literature registry](literature.md#literature-acquisition).
Do not duplicate their authoritative records or create a separate search-log directory.
An unsuccessful search is evidence about the inspected scope, not proof that a result or method does not exist.

A historical checkpoint identifies its date, the assessment or plan it preserves, a commit or artifact version when available, and the link to the current replacement.
Mark it prominently as historical and not a current execution queue.
A routine minor update need not generate a checkpoint.

Preserve failed approaches when they expose an obstruction or prevent repeated work.
Distinguish an unsuccessful attempt, a refuted auxiliary method, and a counterexample to the goal itself.
Reopening a failed route should identify a changed assumption, new mechanism, new evidence, or an error in the earlier assessment; see [strategy conventions](strategies.md).

## Review and Handoff

Keep all formal project-review findings in fixed R under `mathematics/audits/reviews/`, with exact input locators and actual agent relationship/scope, including self-review, independent review, older/source unknown, partial, negative and unadopted findings.
L does not carry a Claim argument; link the actual fixed C reasoning.
An independent finding cannot be relabeled merely to keep it in L.

When correcting a claim, retain the failed statement and its mathematical scope in the correction history.
Repairing its proof does not require a new ID; changing the statement materially does.
Update affected dependencies and assessments in canonical records, publish new versions and maintain affected navigation through ordinary bounded edits or optional rendering.
Strategy changes should cite the evidence that caused them.

## Working drafts and fixed findings

For draft retirement, apply the [common content decision](records.md#substantive-material-retention) first: already fixed mathematics needs no new L or other successor for leftover setup, and genuinely continuing work remains.
Update actual consumers before removing redundant content, with the kind-specific duties intact.
In a personal transfer, complete family collection precedes duplicate reduction; a subagent compares concrete D/fixed-material overlap, and continuing or unresolved work remains even when completed portions are already adequately fixed.

Keep unbound continuing mathematics in `mathematics/drafts/` using canonical rolling `Author_D_F0001.md` identities with the chosen line, through ordinary authorized authoring or the optional `material.py create --kind D --line F --file draft.md` writer.
Both paths use exclusive creation at an available reusable D address, without a fixed-family counter, per-edit versions or a registry.
Fixed support and acceptance evidence must first be promoted to their permanent homes.
Move actual Markdown links, manuscript source maps, program inputs and substantive prose dependencies to the responsible fixed home before removing a completed draft.
Substantial Claim statements and arguments go to C, formal project reviews to R, exploration and ordinary in-discussion checks to L, source inspection to U and programs to J.
A retired D address may be reused without inheriting earlier scope or authority; preserve continuing work and needed programs/data.
Already fixed material never becomes freely editable by moving it into drafts.

For delegated [Preparation](workflow.md#records-review-and-handoff), place concise ordinary prose after the Draft's identity header and before its research body, explaining useful results or methods, decisive conditions and limits, and actual remaining gaps.
Put direct original-material locators beside the relevant judgments, using actual fixed material versions and anchors or source editions with theorem, section or page references as appropriate.
Link adequate detailed materials and retain necessary new analysis in the Draft body or another responsible home, with the existing source and content-retention duties intact.
Reuse still-applicable Preparation and update only what relevant changes or concrete doubts require; no rerun is mandatory each round.
The same subagent may continue research in that Draft within its initial or extended assignment, without copying the Draft or creating a handoff report merely for the assignment change.

Fix valuable failed explorations in ledger when they expose an obstruction, change the route or prevent repeated work, even while the Task remains unfinished.
Preserve the attempted assertion, hypotheses/range, decisive evidence, exact failure or uncertainty, what remains possible and a useful next/reopening condition.
Explain links to existing Tasks/routes, exact Claims/proofs, methods and computations where they matter; add concise return links in their responsible sections.
These are not mandatory fields or automatic proof dependencies.
An inconclusive observation may be fixed without claiming a theorem or adding a review milestone.
Do not invent objects, duplicate narratives or rewrite original dates/attribution for retrospective synthesis.

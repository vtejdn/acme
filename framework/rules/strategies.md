# Research Strategies

## Autonomous Strategy Management

Within an authorized research goal, agents may **add, revise, split, merge, reprioritize, pause, and restart strategies autonomously**.
Do not wait for a separate user request or approval for these ordinary methodological decisions.
A `proposed` strategy is a research candidate, not a request awaiting permission; an agent may activate it when there is a justified next step within scope.
Explicit user constraints continue to govern.

Subagents exercise these choices within their delegated scope and coordinate changes to assignments or resources under the main agent's [research coordination](workflow.md#research-coordination).

Initial priorities for strategies and substrategies are a starting allocation.
Once exploration begins, agents may freely adjust them according to actual findings, obstacles, expected effort and available resources, without first exhausting the initially preferred route or seeking separate approval.
An initial ranking alone is not a continuing constraint; an explicit user requirement to keep a priority or sequence fixed remains binding.
A priority adjustment needs no new mathematical result, S identity or version: update the affected rolling S with its current allocation and necessary reason under the ordinary rules below.

This authority concerns methods and work allocation.
It does not authorize changing the goal's target, quantifiers, completion criteria, or scope beyond the existing authorization.
Strategy dispositions are independent of goal states: pausing a route does not pause or complete its goal, and restarting a route does not activate an unauthorized goal.
A route change does not promote a conjecture or proof draft to a proved claim.

The [result-organization policy](claims.md#lemma-extraction-and-approval) permits internal extraction and proof/dependency organization within an assigned Task without separate approval or lemma labels.
Route management preserves the original objective, actual mathematical review and main-theorem/objective authorization boundaries.

<a id="writable-route-boundary"></a>
Route revisions and predecessor-disposition changes below apply to local writable materials.
Route body and layout maintenance belongs to the same established human under actual authority.
Under the [collaboration rule for locator repairs](identity.md#collaboration-maintenance-and-conflicts), an agent with maintenance authority may repair a path or anchor in another author's local writable S after confirming that the actual target, mathematical meaning and scope of adoption and review are preserved.
Keep the existing S, its author, provenance, evidence, route decisions, Task associations and actual reviews, and repair affected downstream references under that rule.
A change to the mathematical input or the scope of adoption or review follows the responsible author's applicable revision, adoption and review rules.
Publish a substantive contribution under its responsible human's own material identity with exact links, and apply the actual conflict procedure when needed.
When deriving a local successor from a public source material, record the precise source revision or identifiable formulation and the obligations actually carried in the local successor.
Do not change the source route's disposition, priority or history.
A public source material is not writable for local feedback solely because its author key matches the operator.
Explicit references make local derivation discoverable in reverse navigation without implying source-owner adoption or supersession, waiting for a response, or requiring remote synchronization.
The source owner may later adopt the contribution through a real revision.

| Operation | Agent action and responsible content |
| --- | --- |
| Add | Allocate the next unused route ID, link the authorized goal, and state the mechanism, its relation to the goal, inputs and next discriminating step. Set `proposed` or `active` according to the actual work decision. |
| Revise or reprioritize | Keep the ID when the route retains its mathematical identity. Update its scope, allocation or next step and necessary current reason; retain meaningful earlier findings at their responsible homes. A materially different mechanism or target gets a new route ID. |
| Split | Allocate IDs for the distinct successor routes. For local writable predecessors, link predecessor and successors in both directions; keep the original active only if it retains a distinct coordinating role, otherwise mark it `superseded`. For a public source predecessor, link the precise source only from the local successors and preserve its disposition. Record which successors actually carry each unresolved obligation. |
| Merge | Normally create a new route for the combined mechanism and mark local writable predecessors `superseded`. A local writable ID may absorb another local writable route when its mathematical identity remains intact; record the absorption and mark the absorbed route `superseded`. A local combination using a public source route records its source and carried obligations without marking that source absorbed or superseded. Preserve necessary evidence, useful failed findings and unresolved obligations. |
| Pause | Set `paused` when work is intentionally deferred for priority, resource allocation or sequencing. Maintain the current result, unfinished obligation, current reason for pausing and a useful reconsideration condition. State whether the hold includes subroutes. |
| Restart | Return a paused route to `active` when priorities or available resources justify it, recheck its dependencies and maintain the next action. No new theorem or external evidence is required for this allocation decision. Apply the stricter evidence conditions below to blocked, refuted, completed or superseded routes. |

Maintain the current disposition, priority, parent, necessary reason, unfinished obligations and next action in each affected local writable route.
Ordinary allocation, pause/restart or organizational updates need no per-operation agent, timestamp or before/after history.
Retain consequential mechanism changes, useful failures and necessary split/merge lineage with their real known dates, carried obligations and evidence links.
For a local derivation from a public source route, record the source relationship in the local route instead of editing the source.
Link substantive mathematical evidence from the ledger or audits instead of duplicating it.
Do not create a separate approval checklist or require a new audit merely for route administration; mathematical claim changes retain their existing review requirements.

Each route material owns its disposition, priority, Task associations and next step.
Prepared Task associations follow the [common preparation-input responsibility](workflow.md#prepared-work-and-input-applicability), with the agent judging the actual route scope and methodological choices within Task authority.
Update the actual S material and regenerate the derived route index.
Preserve necessary predecessor formulations, carried obligations and cited mathematical locations during split/merge.
Coordinate same-family edits and allocation among concurrent agents.
If every current route is deferred, name the next selection/reassessment step rather than silently changing Task lifecycle.

## Main Strategies and Substrategies

Strategy and Substrategy are roles of the same rolling S material.
The Task owns the exact question and authorization.
A main Strategy explains one coherent mechanism, its major remaining obligations and why its branches are alternatives, jointly needed components, or a mixture.
A Substrategy owns a concrete approach, local uncertainty, next discriminating action and disposition.
Neither the parent nor navigation copies the child's proof or running history.

Use a distinct S when an approach can usefully be selected, compared, assigned, paused or abandoned independently.
A calculation, lemma or local repair within the same mechanism normally remains in that route and its responsible evidence.
Keep the hierarchy shallow unless another level expresses a real research choice.
New notation for an equivalent question is not by itself another mechanism or mathematical progress.

The child declares at most one current `parent` row in its designated `## Route` table.
Its value is one Markdown link to the exact writable local S and an existing stable anchor.
Omit the row for a root; do not use an empty placeholder or root flag.
Self-parenting, duplicate rows, cycles, another material kind and unresolved or ambiguous local identities are invalid.
Examples in literal or excluded regions are not declarations.
Reverse children and Task entrances are derived from the child declarations, not a separate manual registry.

The parent is current organization; a predecessor is historical lineage.
A historical split or matching title, Task, line or serial does not establish a parent.
Reparenting preserves identity and maintains the current parent and any necessary reason or carried obligation, without a dated organizational entry merely for the change.
Cross-route and cross-Task reuse uses precise references rather than additional parents.
Each child retains explicit Task associations and the exact scope served, including any additional Task.
Public source Strategies remain at their original repository/revision/path; a local adaptation uses its own S and an explicit source reference.
S is not an acquired material kind.
Needed external S mathematics may be explicitly adapted by intake into an appropriate fixed receiving home, preserving its actual source scope.
An S associates with an ordinary individual Task, never directly with a joint coordination T.
For a shared goal, use the actual relationship joint T to individual T to that individual's S, compare collaborators' usable routes and failures, and choose useful diversity without forbidding replication.

Ordinary child selection is covered by the existing Task authorization.
A parent link cannot activate an unrelated Task, expand its contract or resume a stopped execution run.
Pausing a parent states whether its children are also paused and updates their actual local dispositions when so; a branch continuing for a separate authorized use states that scope.
Completion and refutation never propagate by counting children: completing the parent requires the actual assembly and completion check, and failure of an auxiliary child need not refute the parent or Task.
A completed source route may be reused without reopening it.

Partial navigation shows an omitted parent as an external context link when available and does not turn it into a new root assignment.
A partial reverse child list does not claim completeness.
Public source references remain explicit repository/revision/path locators; malformed local locators remain errors.

## Identifiers and Files

- Choose and author the rolling S under the [agent/tool responsibility](workflow.md#agent-and-tool-responsibilities).
  The optional `material.py create --kind S --line F --file route.md` writer, or `--task PATH` inheriting only an actual Task's letter, allocates an independent author/origin/S/line numeric sequence, growing from F9999 to F10000.
  No global counter or index reservation owns allocation.
  Preserve the original author and never recycle an allocated identity.
- Local routes use `Author_S_F0001.md`; public source Strategies retain their original repository, fixed revision, path and anchor in the reference.
  They are not installed under a recipient filename.
  Title, priority and associated Task changes do not themselves change identity.
- Put exact Task path/anchor and scope in `## Task associations`; `## Route` owns disposition/priority.
  Link Claims and fixed source inputs precisely.
  A common number/line or filename never implies a Task association.
- Keep the route concise and link full proofs, computations and reviews.
  Its own fields govern route navigation; a derived index row cannot change them.

### Rolling-Document Regime

A strategy file is a rolling document, not a versioned material: it never mints a `name_vN.md` version file.
Keep the same S identity while the core mechanism and intended contribution remain the same.
A new estimate, repaired construction, revised auxiliary parameters or substantial progress does not by itself require a successor.
Update the current question, allocation and next step in place while retaining meaningful earlier failures, consequential expectations and decisions at sufficient responsible homes.
Identity and locator maintenance uses verified current identities without changing historical mathematical meaning.

Allocate another S for a materially different mechanism or independently managed branch, not successive improvements of the same route.
At a consequential split or redirection record the date at its available precision, earlier formulation, what is retained or rejected, and obligations actually carried.
A predecessor is not the previous edit of the same S and does not assert that its current rolling body equals the earlier formulation.
Preserve a cited historical location; if the actual formulation is unavailable, state that limitation.
A concise account of the necessary formulation, reason and inherited obligations with precise evidence links normally suffices.
Use an available Git revision or captured evidence when exact earlier bytes are needed; ordinary maintenance needs no snapshot, hash, receipt or per-edit archive.
Mathematical reliance cites fixed arguments with their actual hypotheses, not a rolling predecessor as proof support.

Ordinary edits require no hash registration or separate maintenance report.
A split or merge preserves meaningful lineage in the routes and maintains affected derived navigation through ordinary bounded edits or optional rendering.

## Choosing and Revising a Route

At the start of an [approved research assignment](workflow.md#from-an-inquiry-to-approved-research), assess the original objective against relevant retained progress.
Read established inputs, provisional premises, promising constructions, useful failures, earlier search findings and source-applicability limits at their responsible homes.
Judge their actual contribution to the target, distinguishing progress from a renamed formulation or another example of the same unresolved mechanism.

Use comprehensive strategy exploration to consider materially relevant mechanisms and perspectives, their relationship to the target and the evidence for choosing among them.
Consider proof and refutation directions when relevant, and alternative representations, constructions or source mechanisms when they can change the decision.
Choose sufficient breadth, necessary depth and investment from the actual question, evidence and effective resources, reusing adequate earlier exploration.
This requires neither every possible route, exhaustive literature coverage, a fixed alternative count, a comparison matrix nor a mandatory agent-S assignment.
An adequate decisive result can resolve the objective without pursuing the remaining alternatives.
Reuse an applicable S under the [route-identity rule](#strategy-hierarchy), separating a route only when its mechanism needs independent selection, comparison, assignment, pause or abandonment.
State its contribution to the Task, actual inputs, provisional obligations, next discriminating investigation and useful continuation or redirection conditions; generic method names and setup alone do not justify route files.

When selecting or materially redirecting a route, search existing Claims, arguments, source audits, constructions, related strategies and failed attempts by the conclusion and problem features actually needed, under [knowledge reuse](knowledge-reuse.md#reuse-before-proof).
Explain consequential reuse at the consuming route: local objects and parameters, satisfied and missing hypotheses, actual output and remaining gap.
Preserve losses and order of choices when they matter.
A simple application can need only one sentence; no separate application record or reciprocal feedback workflow is required.

Before substantial investment in an uncertain new conjecture, a widened assertion or a consequential bridge, consider an inexpensive counterexample or feasibility probe whose outcome could change the research decision.
Choose the test and its necessary precision from the problem, such as a minimal object, degenerate case, scaling, quantifier order, uniformity or compatibility of conditions.
For example, if the route needs an estimate uniform in dimension, first check whether a simple family forces the constant to grow with dimension.
Use adequate existing evidence, a direct argument or an optional computation at the actual scope.
Continue with reliable established inputs or short direct work when a probe would add little, and proceed with a justified approach when no suitable low-cost test is available.

Distinguish a counterexample to the Task's original statement from failure of an auxiliary assertion or one proposed mechanism.
If a probe finds no counterexample, retain its actual range when useful and keep unchecked cases open.
Check a candidate refutation against the exact hypotheses and conclusion, including necessary error control under the [counterexample validation rule](computations.md#counterexample-validation).
Keep useful findings in their responsible mathematical materials and apply [independent-review obligations](proof-audits.md#independent-review-obligations) when actually triggered.
The probe itself requires no new Claim, formal review or delegation and leaves the original Task contract in force.

Before substantial investment in a branch, state what success would provide to the original Task, specifying the objects, assumptions, quantifiers, parameter-choice order and dependencies of constants.
Try to assemble the argument from the Task's original inputs to its conclusion, treating the expected branch result as an explicitly provisional premise.
Reuse an adequate applicable conditional argument, checking the steps that matter to the present allocation.
For this investment judgment, mark the first unsupported step and a few decisive later gaps as explicit provisional premises where needed to derive far enough to identify obstacles that could change the allocation.
Check whether the required objects, quantifiers, uniformity, parameter-choice order, constant dependencies and accumulated losses are jointly compatible, including on previously unresolved inputs.
Identify when a provisional premise may carry the full difficulty of the original problem and state the relationship known so far.

Use the derivation to judge which gaps the branch would reduce and whether a later obstacle changes the value of that work.
Compare the next investigation's possible gains, such as settling a needed premise, ruling out a mechanism or covering new cases, with its actual cost, shared dependencies and viable alternatives.
If neither a positive nor a negative result would change the next decision, reconsider the investigation's scope or priority.
Use qualitative judgment and leave the actual uncertainty explicit.
Limit the derivation to what the current decision needs, and allow bounded conceptual exploration or a discriminating test of early ideas whose bridge is still unclear.
Keep each unresolved premise and the resulting conditional scope of a consequential argument in the appropriate [working Draft or responsible mathematical material](ledger.md#working-drafts-and-fixed-findings), and maintain the allocation choice and reason in the relevant S.
When [actual argument assembly](workflow.md#research-coordination) also applies, reuse the attempt and distinguish the established chain from the conditional continuation.
Adopt mathematical results only at the scope supported by the evidence and required review.

Allocate parallel work by what each successful route would establish for the original Task, the unproved inputs shared by routes, and the complementary parts needed for a complete proof.
Several routes sharing one missing estimate may justify attempts to prove it, bypass it by a different mechanism or find its failure scope; independent complementary lemmas also support useful parallel work.
A construction derived assuming the target remains conditional; an independent construction with a justified sufficient implication back to the target may merit bounded exploration even when its near-term prospects are uncertain.
Choose the number of branches and resources from their mathematical dependencies, distinguishable next steps and actual available limits.
Apply [research coordination](workflow.md#research-coordination) as returns change the allocation and its remaining uses.

When sustained work leaves representative results accumulating without a discernible reduction of the original Task's key gap or a useful next approach, the main agent should delegate a bounded [agent-S exploration](agent-S.md#when-to-delegate-and-assignment-boundaries) within existing authority and resources.
Supply the original question, representative results and their applicability even when no precise bottleneck or alternative is yet known.
User-requested alternatives, consequential allocation choices or new leads can also justify that delegation; neither result counts nor elapsed research time are thresholds.
Progress covering a previously missing case or a clear next proof, calculation or check may instead justify continuing ordinary research assignments.
Reuse still-applicable strategy exploration rather than repeat it merely because more C materials exist.

When several families repeat the same realization or normalization problem, compare a concrete shared construction with the family-specific obligations before allocating work.
Check the complete objects, boundary cases and unused scope of already retained sources.
Similar matrices, scalars or finite coefficients do not establish a structural realization; one campaign supplies experience, not a universal ordering or success rate.
Before assigning finite verification, identify every unbounded parameter and prove that the proposed bounds close all directions.
A bound at each fixed auxiliary layer may leave an infinite family.

Apply the [work-obligation rules](workflow.md#work-obligations).
An exploratory route may state a current question, a mechanism to test and provisional parameter or hypothesis choices; identify what remains to be determined instead of inventing a complete proposition.
When the route undertakes a proof of a particular assertion, state that assertion, hypotheses and quantifiers precisely.
Distinguish an established necessary, sufficient or equivalent relationship from a partial target or heuristic association, and link the justification for any claimed implication or equivalence.

Identify verified inputs separately from conjectural dependencies and unchecked applicability.
Conditional exploration may proceed using those explicitly marked premises without registering or resolving every tentative subclaim first.
State the current uncertainty or obstruction and the next discriminating step: a calculation, candidate assertion, example, or source check whose outcome can change the route's assessment.
Give explicit conditions for continuing, redirecting, or reopening the route.

For a selected investigation, carry it out directly or explicitly assign it under [research follow-up](workflow.md#candidate-research-follow-up), then use the result to choose the next action and update actual work allocation.
Task/S/E preparation or a list of possible strategies alone does not complete approval to explore strategies and advance the objective.
An approval limited to strategy planning retains that deliverable and limit.

A concrete obstruction or promising external mechanism can trigger autonomous literature search under the [repository search policy](literature-search.md#autonomous-literature-search).
Specify the question and how possible findings would affect the route.
Link consequential [search evidence](literature-search.md#search-evidence) at its responsible home, and identify any new lead that justifies expansion or reopening.
Repeated searches with no useful new evidence call for reassessment; stopping a search does not refute the route or discharge an unresolved dependency.

At a consequential redirection or stage closeout, compare the dated expectation with the actual result, explain the discrepancy and its effect on the next decision.
A retrospective synthesis carries its actual date and cannot imply it was consulted earlier.
Complete attempts, calculations and proof arguments remain in their responsible materials; a stable mechanism or lessons section can retain concise reusable experience with exact evidence.
Independent substantive synthesis belongs in appropriate ledger material only when worth preserving separately.
For a received source, record the precise source formulation, applicability and necessary evidence in local writable material; no source amendment or response is required.
Routine actions need no retrospective, and failure of a sufficient construction does not by itself refute its Task.

### Proof and Refutation Direction

After sustained substantive work on an unresolved proposition, reassess the main direction when representative results have not discernibly reduced the central gap, attempts return to the same difficulty and no executable new mechanism offers a way past it.
The main agent gives priority consideration to refuting the original proposition, constructing a counterexample or establishing impossibility.
Judge the direction by actual mathematical progress, grounds for the proposition and the value of the next discriminating investigation, using time and counts only as background.
If proof remains the main direction, identify the mathematical reason, the progress underway or a bounded next investigation that could change the judgment, and carry that choice into the assignments.
For reproduction of a reliable existing proof or work under a human restriction, choose investigations that serve the stated purpose and scope.

An ordinary assignment to prove a proposition permits testing that proposition by counterexample or refutation within the existing method authority and human limits.
Keep the original statement, hypotheses, quantifiers and completion conditions under its [Task contract](tasks.md#task-records).
When the contract allows proof or refutation, a refutation can complete it under the existing evidence and review requirements.
For a proof-only contract, deliver any reliable refutation with its effect on the required proof, and handle the Task's disposition under actual authority and its original completion criteria.

If refutation becomes the main direction, first adjust the selected Task's route use and resource allocation and select or establish the responsible S under the [existing route identity rules](#strategy-hierarchy).
Focus the main effort on a concrete candidate, extreme parameters, a negative argument or counterexample validation, and reduce or defer stalled proof work with its reason and resumption conditions.
Adjust actual delegations, computations and dependencies within the same Task and its effective E limits and stops.
Assign each selected [next investigation](workflow.md#candidate-research-follow-up) to an executor with a discriminating question and a point for returning the result.
For a shared S, record each Task's use, allocation, deferral scope and resumption conditions in its [Task associations](../material-format.md#route-and-feedback-tables) `Scope` and necessary prose.
The `Route` disposition and priority describe the whole route, which stays `active` while another Task continues current work on it.
Split uses that need independent management under the existing strategy organization rules and link their common mathematical evidence.

Determine the exact negation from all original hypotheses, the order of quantifiers, parameter dependencies and convergence requirements before selecting the objects and scope of the investigation.
For example, to negate that every admissible x has a suitable y, find one admissible x for which every allowed y fails.
For existence, uniform estimates or limits, use the object families and bounds that the actual negation requires, and state each partial result's scope and remaining obligations.
Distinguish refuting the original proposition, refuting an auxiliary assertion and finding an obstacle to a mechanism.
Verify a candidate against the original hypotheses and its logical contradiction of the conclusion, applying [counterexample validation](computations.md#counterexample-validation) to numerical candidates and retaining applicable error control and review.
Keep the verified refutation and precise scope with the original proposition's responsible evidence under the [mathematical assessment rules](claims.md#separate-assessment-fields).

After investigation, use the candidate, structural restriction, effective input or remaining gap to decide whether to continue refutation, return to proof or explore a different mechanism.
When returning to proof or changing direction again, identify the changed mathematical understanding, mechanism or actual conditions and the next investigation whose result now matters.
Apply the distinct [restart conditions](#route-dispositions) for an allocation pause, an unmet prerequisite and a refuted route.
If a search yields no counterexample, report its actual range, results and unchecked space, and base the proposition's assessment on sufficient mathematical evidence.
When neither direction has a useful next step, choose an informative investigation of an obstruction, representation or mechanism within actual authority and available resources.
Maintain the current direction, reason, next action and resumption conditions for the actual Task use in S, and keep complete derivations, computations and useful failures at their responsible homes.

<a id="route-dispositions"></a>
### Dispositions and Restart Conditions

| Disposition | Meaning and condition for changing it |
| --- | --- |
| `proposed` | A candidate mechanism not yet selected for execution. Activate autonomously when it offers an in-scope next step. |
| `active` | Selected for current work, including a coordinating route with active subroutes. It need not have a tool or agent running at every moment. |
| `paused` | Intentional deferral. Resume for a recorded priority, sequencing or resource reason after checking the current context; the underlying mathematics need not have changed. |
| `blocked` | A named unmet prerequisite prevents the planned next step. Resume when that prerequisite changes, a justified alternative bypasses it, or the earlier diagnosis is corrected. If investigating the missing lemma is itself an executable next step, its being unproved alone does not require a blocked disposition. |
| `completed` | The route's stated deliverable is achieved with the required evidence. Reopen the same route if a concrete challenge reveals an unmet completion obligation; broader follow-up work normally gets a new route. Route completion need not complete the goal. |
| `refuted` | Evidence rules out the recorded mechanism or auxiliary assertion under its stated hypotheses. Reconsider only with a concrete change in evidence, assumptions, mechanism or diagnosis. Preserve the failed assertion; it remains refuted unless its refutation is overturned. A materially different replacement gets a successor ID. |
| `superseded` | Work has been reorganized into identified successors or absorbed elsewhere. Keep the disposition, necessary mathematical record and successor links. Restore the same ID only when deliberately reversing that reorganization while reinstating the same route identity; reconcile successor dispositions to avoid duplicate active ownership. |

Link sufficient failure scope and evidence at its responsible L/J/C/R home; the S retains its disposition, reason and next action without duplicating that evidence.
Failure of a sufficient strategy does not refute its goal.
A **goal counterexample** requires a verified example contradicting the original goal under its actual hypotheses, with the relevant claim and proof review linked before the goal's assessment changes.
Do not confuse an allocation decision to pause with evidence that a strategy is blocked or refuted, or treat restarting work as proof that an earlier mathematical objection is resolved.

## Record Template

Use the inert [record template](../templates/mathematics/strategy.md), replacing placeholders with actual evidence.
A template is neither authorization nor a completed record.

When a useful failed attempt changes a route or prevents repetition, preserve its scope, evidence and limitations without waiting for Task completion, reusing adequate responsible material and adding L only for necessary exploration or synthesis.
Link that finding from the route evidence/change section and explain its consequence for continuation or reopening.
Keep ongoing unbound arguments in mathematics/drafts/; strategy decisions remain in this rolling record.

## Strategy navigation

The existing renderer derives mathematics/strategies/index.md from S Route declarations and actual source/feedback relationships: route identity/title, parent hierarchy, Task association and route disposition.
S and the exact linked T/evidence own those meanings.
After relevant inputs settle, maintain affected navigation through ordinary bounded edits or optional rendering, including populated generated rows.
Preserve route values, exact links and source facts; changing the index cannot change the responsible S, T or evidence.

# Personal Policies and Writing Choices

Apply policy scope through the [agent/tool responsibility](workflow.md#agent-and-tool-responsibilities).
A policy locator reports the responsible files; it does not interpret instructions, establish permission or adopt another author's choices.

<a id="scope-and-adoption"></a>
## Selection and Scope

For writing, apply exactly four layers, from lowest to highest: general mathematical writing rules; the responsible author's author-wide personal policy; that author's applicable subfield policy; the manuscript-specific choice.
This order governs notation, terminology, definition presentation, source/model selection and style alike.
A higher applicable layer overrides the conflicting portion and inherits everything else.
No repeated approval is needed.
Source observations in responsible source-reading records are evidence for choices, not a fifth layer; definitions, terminology and symbols use the common def/source workflow.
The current author's new instruction updates the layer and scope it actually names; a paper-specific request never becomes author-wide automatically.

Personal defaults live under profiles/<human>/, with writing/readme.md and actual named subfield files where useful.
Read the manuscript's responsible author and actual topic/readership, not merely the bound operator or Task line.
Changing a local actor does not alter another author's adopted manuscript choices.
An author without a profile uses general mathematical standards plus any explicit manuscript choices.
Do not create empty profiles, borrow another author's settings or ask for routine missing defaults.

For non-writing defaults and delegated choices within the installed framework's permitted scope, use the current explicit instruction, then an explicit Task/document choice, then the bound operator's applicable current active policy, then that operator's default, then the common framework default.
This selects ordinary method/route preferences and permitted execution limits; a higher applicable choice overrides only the conflicting selectable portion without routine exception approval.
Personal defaults, active policies and document choices do not by themselves waive truthful mathematics/attribution, fixed-version and exact-source preservation, applicable acceptance/review evidence, actual author/role and Task/E authority, stopped conditions or private-source/publication boundaries.
Writing retains its four layers for notation, terminology, presentation, source/model choice and style; those choices cannot turn an unchecked citation into verified evidence or change another material's assessment.
Task/decision/E changes use their existing scoped procedures and actual authority; framework-obligation changes require a separately explicit maintenance assignment under its actual role and scope.
Current human instructions retain their actual authority, including an already authorized named role exception, without transferring authorship or rewriting past evidence.
The operator comes from the existing Git worktree research.actor binding and repository/people.json, not a Task's author, Git display name or agent nickname.
Profiles are selected automatically without a separate repository adoption.
A checkout has one binding shared by its chats; it is not a per-chat identity.
Work directly in the shared repository and existing checkout under the [collaboration boundary](identity.md#collaboration-maintenance-and-conflicts).
The actual operator binding supplies no authority to adopt another author's policies or take over their materials.

<a id="instruction-scope"></a>
## Classify the actual instruction

First identify the requested action and scope.
A correction of an existing rule's application calls for re-reading that rule and repairing the authorized work; it does not create a personal preference or an upgrade record.
An explicit individual preference belongs in the existing applicable profile topic or document choice.
A changed Task target, hypotheses or execution conditions belongs in the responsible T/decision/E procedure.
Do not invent a profile or use one to store common framework semantics.

Ordinary released installations use their installed rules by default.
Research requests, corrections, check failures and preferences do not authorize framework edits, newer-framework retrieval or version changes.
Explain a suggested common-rule difference at its actual scope and continue authorized work where possible.
A separately explicit framework-maintenance assignment retains its own scope and actual responsibilities; an agent's name or a directory's presence does not assign that responsibility.

## Defaults and Active Policy

A topic may contain Default policy and, only when needed, Current active policy.
State the applicable scope and any condition needed to determine whether an active rule applies.
Active/default precedence operates within its layer; it never outranks an applicable manuscript choice from a higher writing layer.
Override only specified fields.
Expiry restores the remaining applicable default prospectively; it neither resumes a stopped E nor resets its allowance.
Do not invent an expiry, policy ID, activation receipt, JSON registry or permission workflow.
A genuinely unresolved same-layer conflict needs only the missing scope/choice clarified.

<a id="recording-and-updating"></a>
## Recording Choices

Record explicit human instructions or approved proposals promptly in English as individual entries at the responsible scope.
Each entry states the current rule and its applicable scope.
Do not record instruction dates, adoption or supersession history, or administrative metadata.
Update the affected entry directly when a rule changes.
Questions, examples, silence and an agent preference do not create a policy.
Keep manuscript-specific choices with their manuscript; source observations stay in responsible source-reading accounts.
Bibliographic facts belong to the canonical references.bib, and artifact facts belong to the literature registry.

Clean exports contain common guidance and empty mathematical structures, not live personal policies, named source selections, budgets or execution authorization.
Explicit personal transfer retains the person and applicable scope; copying a policy grants no Task execution authority.

# Personal Policies and Writing Choices

Apply policy scope through the [agent/tool responsibility](workflow.md#agent-and-tool-responsibilities).
A policy locator reports the responsible files; it does not interpret instructions, establish permission or adopt another author's choices.

<a id="scope-and-adoption"></a>
## Selection and Scope

For single-writer documents, apply exactly four writing layers, from lowest to highest: general mathematical writing rules; the responsible writer's author-wide personal policy; that writer's applicable subfield policy; the manuscript-specific choice.
This order governs notation, terminology, definition presentation, source/model selection and style alike.
A higher applicable layer overrides the conflicting portion and inherits everything else.
Apply the author's writing choices within the [repository writing language rule](organization.md#repository-writing-language).
No repeated approval is needed.
Source observations in responsible source-reading records are evidence for choices, not a fifth layer; definitions, terminology and symbols use the common def/source workflow.
The current author's new instruction updates the layer and scope it actually names; a paper-specific request never becomes author-wide automatically.
Current manuscript usage establishes the text being maintained, not an additional policy layer or approval to depart from an effective choice.

For [explicit joint writing](writing.md#explicit-joint-writing), use the existing shared manuscript choices and common framework defaults.
An individual's author-wide or subfield choices apply to that shared document only when actually adopted for it; retain adopted common choices in its existing writing/conventions home under the same four layers.
This creates no fifth layer, automatic profile merge, group policy register or duty to obtain every writer's private profile.
Changing the current operator does not switch the shared document's effective choices, and the associated Task's author does not supply the writing policy.
Without an applicable shared PDF-tracking choice, retain the common local-output defaults rather than take the union of individual exceptions.

Personal defaults live under profiles/<human>/, with writing/readme.md and actual named subfield files where useful.
The [personal guidance layout](#personal-communication-and-writing-guidance) separates explicit preferences, advisory notes and shared terminology without adding a writing layer.
The author's message-archive.md is historical correspondence, not personal policy; its location or existence does not select it for startup or independent-review context.
Read the manuscript's actual responsible writer(s), effective choices and topic/readership, not merely the bound operator or Task line.
Changing a local actor does not alter another author's adopted manuscript choices.
An author without a profile uses general mathematical standards plus any explicit manuscript choices.
An author may explicitly choose tracking exceptions for their document PDFs at the applicable writing-profile or document scope under the [local-output rule](writing.md#files-kept-local).
Those choices do not waive local-only non-PDF build products or private literature exclusions, do not apply to another author and do not authorize commit, push or publication.
Do not create empty profiles, borrow another author's settings or ask for routine missing defaults.

For non-writing defaults and delegated choices within the installed framework's permitted scope, use the current explicit instruction, then an explicit Task/document choice, then the bound operator's applicable current active policy, then that operator's default, then the common framework default.
This selects ordinary method/route preferences and permitted execution limits; a higher applicable choice overrides only the conflicting selectable portion without routine exception approval.
Personal defaults, active policies and document choices do not by themselves waive truthful mathematics/attribution, fixed-version and exact-source preservation, applicable acceptance/review evidence, actual author/role and Task/E authority, stopped conditions or private-source/publication boundaries.
Writing retains its four layers for notation, terminology, presentation, source/model choice and style; those choices cannot turn an unchecked citation into verified evidence or change another material's assessment.
Task/decision/E changes use their existing scoped procedures and actual authority; framework-obligation changes require a separately explicit maintenance assignment under its actual role and scope.
Current human instructions retain their actual authority, including an already authorized named role exception, without transferring authorship or rewriting past evidence.
In a Git workspace, the operator comes from the existing worktree research.actor binding and repository/people.json, not a Task's author, Git display name or agent nickname.
Without Git, use the currently explicitly identified human and existing registration under the [identity rule](identity.md#stable-keys-and-local-attribution).
Applicable individual profiles are selected automatically without a separate repository adoption, subject to the joint-document adoption boundary above.
A checkout has one binding shared by its chats; it is not a per-chat identity.
Work directly in the shared repository and existing checkout under the [collaboration boundary](identity.md#collaboration-maintenance-and-conflicts).
The actual operator binding supplies no authority to adopt another author's policies or take over their materials.

<a id="instruction-scope"></a>
## Classify the actual instruction

First identify the requested action and scope.
A correction of an existing rule's application calls for re-reading that rule and repairing the authorized work; it does not create a personal preference or an upgrade record.
An explicit individual preference belongs in the existing applicable profile topic or document choice.
A changed Task target, hypotheses or execution conditions belongs in the responsible T/decision/E procedure.
Do not invent a profile or use one to store common framework semantics.

Ordinary released installations use their installed rules by default.
Research requests, corrections, check failures and preferences do not authorize framework edits, replacement-framework retrieval or version changes.
The [daily public version check](../versioning.md#official-version-check) is a narrow read-only metadata exception during ordinary work, subject to explicit offline instructions and the repository setting.
It neither authorizes retrieval or application of replacement files nor changes the installed version, mathematical status or Task/E authority.
Explain a suggested common-rule difference at its actual scope and continue authorized work where possible.
A separately explicit framework-maintenance assignment retains its own scope and actual responsibilities; an agent's name or a directory's presence does not assign that responsibility.

## Personal communication and writing guidance

### Responsible homes and precedence

Use the following matching communication and writing layout under `profiles/<human>/` only when there is actual content to retain.

| Profile-relative path | Responsible content |
| --- | --- |
| `communication/readme.md` | Explicit author preferences for dialogue, with their actual scope. |
| `communication/notes.md` | Agent-derived general guidance for communicating with this author. |
| `writing/readme.md` and existing named subfield files | Explicit author writing preferences at their existing applicable scope. |
| `writing/notes.md` | Agent-derived general writing guidance, with necessary field, audience or document limits. |
| `terminology.md` | Shared personal terminology, notation and related mathematical expression choices used by conversation and writing. |

First determine effective human choices under the [actual instruction scope](#instruction-scope) and [existing precedence and four writing layers](#scope-and-adoption), then use agent notes within the remaining permitted discretion.
Applicable explicit author preferences always prevail over conflicting agent summaries.
Agent notes remain advisory and create no additional policy layer, adopted author choice or permission.
Only an actual explicit human instruction or adoption changes the responsible preference; retain an adopted choice at its proper home and remove redundant note content.
Keep a one-off request within that exchange unless the actual instruction adopts a lasting preference, and correct the application of an existing rule without creating a new personal preference.

Maintain shared terminology and notation once in `profiles/<human>/terminology.md`, preserving actual scope and clearly distinguishing explicit author choices from advisory agent suggestions when both are retained.
Communication and writing files may point to the relevant section instead of copying its list; agent summaries cannot silently change an explicit convention.
This shared file stays within the existing author and subfield choice scope, preserving the precedence of a more specific applicable manuscript choice.
Keep manuscript-specific choices at their document home, including when discussing that manuscript, rather than promoting them into general personal terminology.
Mathematical definitions, source facts and their evidence retain their existing mathematical or source homes; this file owns expression choices rather than another definition catalog.

### Active summarization and rolling maintenance

During actual dialogue, writing and revision, proactively extract useful lasting guidance from feedback and experience and update the responsible notes without routine per-entry approval.
Summarize retained experience as clear, general current guidance with only the scope needed for correct use.
Revise, combine or remove entries as understanding improves, consolidating overlapping lessons rather than appending one entry after each exchange or revision.
Keep no accumulating transcript, incident list, correction history, dated learning log or score, and impose no arbitrary note-size quota.
Retain no observation that supplies no useful general guidance and create no empty profile, note or section.
Record explicit author instructions at their preference home rather than as agent inference; draft usage, silence and agent recommendations establish no explicit preference.

### Reading and tool boundaries

For dialogue under the [conversation norm](organization.md#mathematical-conversation), read the actual human's applicable communication guidance; for writing, read the actual responsible writer's applicable choices and notes, using relevant shared terminology in either case.
Reuse adequate current-context reading and select necessary sections without loading every profile file for each response.
Absent notes leave the common defaults usable, without a startup creation or whole-profile reading requirement.
Before revision, read the current manuscript and its effective choices under [current-manuscript authority](writing.md#current-manuscript-authority); an experience note cannot restore outdated text or override that actual material and its effective choices.
Joint writing retains its adopted shared choices without automatically importing an operator's personal preferences or notes when the operator changes.
Keep shared notes suitable for the [existing profile visibility](organization.md#machine-local-locators), retaining concise nonsensitive guidance rather than raw private exchanges or speculation about the author.
Mathematical evidence, research status and execution authority retain their responsible homes.
[Independent review](proof-audits.md#review-arrangements) receives only its necessary scoped instructions rather than automatic conversation-memory context.
An optional policy locator may retain its narrow explicit-path output; that output neither replaces reading necessary shared terminology and notes nor ranks advisory notes as human policy.
The agent judges terminology, explanation, useful generalization and consolidation through ordinary reading and editing, without a memory service, classifier, semantic checker, learning register or mandatory tool sequence.

### Bounded adaptation of existing profiles

When an affected profile contains the former mixed `profiles/<human>/communication.md`, read it and move useful explicit preferences to `communication/readme.md` and consolidated agent experience to `communication/notes.md`.
Move shared terminology and notation from affected communication or writing locations to `terminology.md`, preserving actual scope and the distinction between explicit author choices and agent suggestions without duplicate lists.
Do not label content of uncertain origin as an explicit author choice without the necessary actual evidence.
Repair actual references and confirm useful content is retained at its responsible homes before removing the retired flat file and redundant entries.
Complete necessary bounded adaptation before marking an upgraded target version installed, without old-path aliases, compatibility readers, transcript processing or an unrelated profile scan.

## Defaults and Active Policy

A topic may contain Default policy and, only when needed, Current active policy.
State the applicable scope and any condition needed to determine whether an active rule applies.
Active/default precedence operates within its layer; it never outranks an applicable manuscript choice from a higher writing layer.
Override only specified fields.
Expiry restores the remaining applicable default prospectively; it neither resumes a stopped E nor resets its allowance.
Do not invent an expiry, policy ID, activation receipt, JSON registry or permission workflow.
A genuinely unresolved same-layer conflict needs only the missing scope/choice clarified.

<a id="recording-and-updating"></a>
## Recording Choices

Record explicit human instructions or approved proposals promptly as individual entries at the responsible scope.
Follow the [repository writing language rule](organization.md#repository-writing-language) when writing those entries.
Each entry states the current rule and its applicable scope.
Do not record instruction dates, adoption or supersession history, or administrative metadata.
Update the affected entry directly when a rule changes.
Questions, examples, silence and an agent preference do not create a policy.
Update a lagging manuscript choice only from actual explicit human instructions or adopted choices at that scope; neither current draft usage, historical opinions nor reviewer suggestions justify changing policy to fit the text.
Use the existing writing layers to resolve an applicable text/choice conflict, clarifying only a decisive same-layer, intent or authority gap while continuing independent authorized work.
Keep manuscript-specific choices with their manuscript; source observations stay in responsible source-reading accounts.
Bibliographic facts belong to canonical references.bib; useful edition/source notes belong to the source README, and inspected mathematics and limits belong to U or adequate C/L.

The official starting repository contains common guidance and empty mathematical structures, without live personal policies, named source selections, budgets or execution authorization.
Explicit personal transfer retains the person and applicable scope; copying a policy grants no Task execution authority.
The [personal-import path](intake.md#personal-import) installs only explicitly selected applicable settings in that actual human's existing responsible home, resolving conflicts from current precedence without directory-wide copying or revival of expired permission.

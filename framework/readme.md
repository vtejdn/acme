# ACME: Autonomous and Collaborative Mathematical Exploration

ACME is the Autonomous and Collaborative Mathematical Exploration framework (fixed identity: acme).
Autonomous research operates within human-assigned objectives and authorization.
Collaborative work follows the explicitly agreed scope.
Framework identity is independent of each repository's own name and locally registered external correspondences.

This directory owns portable rules, inert material templates and the explicit export contract.
It contains no live research status, bibliographic registry or selected manuscript.

The current release identifier is maintained in the [export manifest](export-manifest.json) under the [versioning rules](versioning.md).
The [start-of-work action](agent-actions.md#start-a-work-round) applies [current-rule reading](rules/workflow.md#proportionate-execution) independently of daily public-version checks and any upgrade reminder.

## Core principles and interpretation

These principles apply to all agent actions.
Use them when framework provisions conflict, omit a case or admit multiple readings, and whenever a concrete matter requires agent discretion.
Interpret and maintain topic rules as implementations of these principles.
Within current explicit human instructions and actual authority, choose the course that advances mathematical research, preserves necessary substance and completes the work with the least administrative burden.
Act directly when these principles and the available facts determine an adequate choice; clarify only a remaining decisive gap in human intent, authority or facts.
Applying the principles creates no new approval, exception register or per-action justification requirement.

1. **Mathematical research first.**
   Help humans formulate, understand, advance and complete mathematical research.
   Judge rules, tools and documents by mathematical quality, useful continuation and actual collaboration needs, without substituting artifact counts, complete fields or completed procedures for research progress.
2. **Minimal administration.**
   Keep only structures and steps needed to support research, clarify responsibility and protect necessary evidence and actual authority.
   Prefer simple direct arrangements, reuse existing materials and remove redundant registration, routine reports, acknowledgments, extra approvals, obsolete mechanisms and disposable byproducts.
   Simplification itself creates no new form, approval or record-keeping duty.
3. **Retain substance, not process history.**
   Preserve precise questions, necessary hypotheses and arguments, useful failures and counterexamples, required programs and inputs, and actual source and review facts and limits.
   Judge historical records by their actual mathematical, evidentiary, reproduction or current execution purpose.
   Compatibility, old formats and old references alone do not justify retaining non-substantive content.
   Authorization history may likewise be shortened or removed; keep enough information to determine currently effective authority and limits without keeping the full process that produced them.
   Preserve necessary content and repair affected consumers before removing redundant containers; removed process needs no historical summary, archive copy or replacement record.
4. **One responsible home, with reuse first.**
   Maintain each mathematical content item, status, source fact and decision at its responsible home.
   Cite and reuse adequate applicable materials instead of duplicating proofs, reviews or synchronized accounts of the same fact.
   Navigation and summaries help locate responsible materials without becoming another status, evidence or authority source.
5. **Agent judgment, simple tools.**
   The agent reads actual materials and judges content, source applicability, necessary retention, mathematical sufficiency and action scope.
   Tools assist retrieval, computation and explicit mechanical operations, reporting what they actually check without replacing content judgment, mathematical review or authority.
   Keep useful automation and concrete mechanical protections without building complex text-inference systems or accumulating semantic exceptions.
6. **Action and verification proportionate to actual need.**
   Work on the actual question, authority and affected scope, completing necessary work and checks without an automatic whole-library cleanup or historical rerun.
   Assume trusted collaboration and use checks to detect concrete errors, proportionately to their impact and unresolved uncertainties.
   Reuse adequate still-applicable reading, computations and reviews; expand or repeat checks only for relevant changes, failures, concrete doubts or an explicit request.

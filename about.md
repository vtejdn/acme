# About
Our framework supports mathematical research through collaboration between human researchers and autonomous AI agents.
Its goal is to explore the frontier of mathematis, develop precise questions, and preserve research that can be inspected, reused, and continued.

## Human
Human researchers define the research objectives and authorized scope, clarify mathematical intent, and guide scientific priorities.
They contribute ideas, challenge arguments, and make key decisions about theorem selection, manuscript direction, and exposition.
They can intervene throughout the process to refine questions or redirect the investigation.
## AI agent
AI agents carry out sustained research within that scope: studying and verifying literature, developing and revising strategies, constructing proofs and counterexamples, running reproducible computations, and preparing manuscripts.
Agents also review arguments, identify gaps, and maintain records of assumptions, dependencies, unsuccessful approaches, and unresolved questions.
Routine methodological decisions can proceed autonomously, while designated changes requiring human approval remain explicit.
## Workflow
The workflow connects goal definition → literature verification → strategy development → proof and computation → review → manuscript preparation, with feedback between stages.
Stable claim identifiers and linked evidence support collaboration across agents and sessions.
The framework distinguishes conjectures, computational observations, proof drafts, and reviewed results, enabling theorem reproduction and generalization while keeping the strength and limitations of each conclusion visible.

# Manuscripts

See the [current index](index.md) and [framework rules](../../framework/readme.md).

Manuscripts use TeX under the [supported writing scope](../../framework/rules/writing.md#tex-manuscripts-and-supporting-records), with human authors guiding agent writing from those sources or editing them directly.
Markdown supplies agent-facing project instructions, explanations, choices, citation/review records and necessary working documents rather than alternative manuscripts.
Writing or reviewing a manuscript selects its actual current TeX sources; delivering Markdown prose alone does not complete a TeX manuscript assignment.

Use the action matching the assignment to write, revise or review a manuscript.
See [Write or revise a manuscript](../../framework/agent-actions.md#write-or-revise-a-manuscript) and [Review a manuscript](../../framework/agent-actions.md#review-a-manuscript).
Writing self-checks and writing review use the same standards.
See the [common manuscript checks](../../framework/rules/writing.md#manuscript-writing-rule-checks).
Use the [selected manuscript's revision list](../../framework/rules/writing.md#manuscript-revisions) for writing opinions, including the rule for carrying forward attributable opinions from a prior single-manuscript list.
Use the [mathematical-review home](../../framework/rules/writing.md#manuscript-review-and-mathematical-provenance) for proof findings, including proof-only revisions.
Use the appropriate [companion records](../../framework/rules/writing.md#project-and-document-records) for citations and writing choices.

Create a manuscript project only when authorized writing needs it.
Use `mathematics/manuscripts/<writing-author>/<full-task-family>/` for a Task-associated project under the [writing-home rule](../../framework/rules/writing.md#projects-and-documents), preserving the Task's own complete identity without its physical version suffix.
Any authorized writer can write for another human's ordinary Task or a joint Task without a personal participation Task or matching number, including a writer with no personal Task.
For example, writer `Alice` can use `mathematics/manuscripts/Alice/Bob_T_A0002/paper/main.tex`.
Use a writer container and purpose-named project when there is no corresponding Task, without manufacturing T/E.
An [explicit joint-writing assignment](../../framework/rules/writing.md#explicit-joint-writing) can use `Bob_Alice/Bob_T_A0002/` for its shared document and local records, without registering the joined label as a human or inferring a byline.
Reuse one actual manuscript home rather than synchronized per-writer copies, preserving effective shared choices and actual mathematical attribution.
Existing projects undergo the [required upgrade migration](../../framework/rules/writing.md#upgrading-existing-writing-projects), including necessary local files, build paths and references before version advancement; retired homes have no compatibility aliases.
Manuscript projects and another human's ordinary Task/E execution authority are outside mathematical intake.
A necessary fixed joint coordination contract may be received for actual continued collaboration under the [joint-Task conditions](../../framework/rules/tasks.md#joint-tasks-and-participation), with no E or adoption of another participant's Task, strategies or execution authority.
Your own selected Tasks and manuscript sources, settings and necessary dependencies may be imported under the [personal-import rule](../../framework/rules/intake.md#personal-import), preserving authorship and effective execution conditions.
Importing your own manuscript creates no unrequested Task or execution authority.
Put the title, actual responsible writer(s), writing assignment and scope, purpose, necessary mathematical inputs and document entrances in its README or adequate document record.
Include a Task only when it actually exists and applies, and build entries only when relevant.
An assignment limited to supporting agent-facing records needs no unrequested TeX document or compilation.
Standard children such as `paper/main.tex` use the project's actual writing responsibility without changing publication bylines or repeating the root name.

For citation labels, author-name display, sorting and reference-list structure, consult the [AMS, EMS and LMS bibliography guidance](bibliography-guidelines.md).
Apply the actual author and manuscript choices when selecting among the documented schemes.

Before relying on needed literature, follow the [common source-preparation workflow](../../framework/rules/literature.md#source-preparation), register complete definitions through the [concept index](../definitions/index.md), then apply the actual author and manuscript choices.
Stylistic and bibliography-only uses retain proportionate registration and inspection duties; manuscript citation maps include only citations actually present in the TeX.

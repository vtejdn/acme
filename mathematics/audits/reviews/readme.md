# Audits Proofs

Before recording R, apply the [common content decision](../../../framework/rules/records.md#substantive-material-retention), then the review obligations.
An actual new review of fixed mathematics may add necessary evidence; retain exact inputs, finding, relationship and limits, including partial/negative results.
Process announcements and link checks are not such reviews.
Follow the [kind-specific rules](../../../framework/rules/proof-audits.md#review-scope-and-records).

Independent project-review accounts belong to fixed R here, including partial, negative, unresolved and unadopted findings.
Record actual inputs, scope and relationship; location alone does not establish independence.
Suitable self-review or unknown findings may be in R or fixed L.
Source inspection remains in U and checker programs/data remain in J.
See the [review rules](../../../framework/rules/proof-audits.md).

See the [current index](index.md) and [framework rules](../../../framework/readme.md).

Manuscript-specific review belongs with its document under the [manuscript review rules](../../../framework/rules/writing.md#manuscript-review-and-mathematical-provenance).
Project-mathematics R findings retain their actual C/T/computation targets and coverage.

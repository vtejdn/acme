### [ProofForum](https://www.proofforum.org/)

ProofForum accepts AI-generated mathematical work and records human verification and substantive contributions such as proof repairs and new lemmas. [Philosophy, aims & scope](https://www.proofforum.org/philosophy)
We use ProofForum for our human-authored work to emphasize the human understanding, verification, and further development of agent-generated material.

## By agents

We regard any mathematical material generated by AI agents as a shared resource for the mathematical community.
Therefore we claim no priority or credit for the raw mathematical outputs in this repository.
If you find any of the material in this repository useful for your own research, feel free to use, adapt, or build on it.
A brief acknowledgment of the source would be appreciated.

### [Hexagon](https://hexagonmath.org/)

Hexagon's founding executive director, Benjamin Antieau, states in its launch announcement: “Hexagon specifically allows submissions entirely generated by LLMs and even where the submitters claim no human understanding.” [Launch announcement](https://antieau.github.io/2026/10/05/hexagon.html)
We use Hexagon for agent-generated work to make raw mathematical outputs available for the community to study, adapt, and build on.

### [Palomar](https://palomar-registry.org/)

Palomar allows AI-generated formalizations and records Lean proof checks and automated review for a fixed source version. [About Palomar](https://palomar-registry.org/about)
We use Palomar for agent-generated work as a way to register and share formal proofs with their precise statements and recorded checks.
